1. Bank audit — numbers to remember

PointRule
NPA (non-performing asset) — bankInterest / instalment overdue more than 90 days. Dating: due date D unpaid → overdue from D; SMA-1 at D+30, SMA-2 at D+60, NPA at D+90 (SMA = special mention account).
Classification basisBorrower-wise, not facility-wise — one facility NPA → all NPA. On past-due record, not just the 31 March balance.
Limit not reviewed / renewedNot reviewed within 180 days of due date → NPA. Renewal letter issued after 31 March but before audit report = adjusting event (AS 4) → standard.
Drawing power (DP)Stock statement used for DP not older than 3 months. Temporary extra limit up to 20% of limit for max 90 days. Temporary DP shortfall alone ≠ NPA.
Central vs State Government guaranteeCentral-guaranteed loan becomes NPA only when guarantee invoked and repudiated; State-guaranteed → NPA after 90 days like any loan. Income on NPA only when realised.
RestructuringStandard → becomes sub-standard on restructuring; NPA stays in same category; no retrospective restructuring.
Sale of NPASeller: only NPAs at least 2 years old in its books; without recourse; cash, full price upfront; price below net book value → loss to P&L; above → excess provision not reversed. Buyer: recoveries first against cost; 100% risk weight.
Capital (CRAR = capital to risk-weighted assets ratio)Banks: minimum 9% ongoing. Illustration risk weights: housing loan up to ₹75 lakh 50%, above ₹75 lakh 75%, commercial real estate 100%.
CRR / SLR / DTLCRR (cash reserve ratio) = minimum % of deposits kept as cash/deposit with RBI (BR Act for banking companies, RBI Act for scheduled banks). SLR (statutory liquidity ratio) = liquid assets (cash, gold, approved securities) before lending — s.24 BR Act. Central auditors verify on odd dates in different months, not Fridays; Form A return (CRR), Form VIII (SLR); report to top management and RBI. DTL (demand and time liabilities): exclude capital, reserves, RBI loans, refinance (EXIM/NHB/NABARD/SIDBI), part-recoveries on bad debts, margins on funded facilities; include interest accrued on deposits (booked or not), net credit balance in branch adjustment, adverse Nostro mirror balance.
Other bank limitsInward clearing cheque ₹5 lakh+ → branch calls customer. Bulk deposit = ₹2 crore+. Dormant account = no transaction over 2 years. s.9 BR Act: non-banking immovable property not held over 7 years unless own use. s.15 BR Act: no dividend until adequate bad-debt provision. Investments: HTM / HFT / AFS at acquisition; AFS → HTM needs Board approval; non-performing investment = due over 90 days. RBI balance: entries unresponded over 15 days.
AppointmentBanking company → shareholders at AGM; nationalised bank → its Board; prior RBI approval both. Central statutory auditors = 4+ firms jointly; private banks: no mandatory branch audit. Plus LFAR (long form audit report).

Bank provisioning % rates are not in the Final SM (it refers to Intermediate). Don't mix with NBFC rates below.

2. NBFC audit — numbers to remember

PointRule
Registration and NOF (net owned fund)s.45-IA RBI Act: certificate of registration (CoR) + NOF. New NBFCs NOF ₹10 crore. Glide path to ₹10 crore by 31 March 2027 for ICC, MFI, Factor (ICC ₹2 → ₹5 cr by Mar 2025 → ₹10 cr by Mar 2027). From 1 July 2026: no public funds + no customer interface + assets below ₹1,000 cr → exempt (Type I – Exempt, may deregister by 31 Dec 2026); above ₹1,000 cr → Type I registered; public funds or customer interface → Type II registered.
LayersBase: non-deposit, assets below ₹1,000 cr (and always P2P, AA). Middle: all deposit-taking (any size), non-deposit ₹1,000 cr+, HFCs, CICs, IFCs. Upper: identified by RBI; top 10 by size. Top: ideally empty.
NPA — NBFCGlide path: over 90 days from 31 March 2026 (150 days 2024, 120 days 2025). Sub-standard: NPA up to 18 months (Base) / 12 months (Middle+); then doubtful.
ProvisionsSub-standard 10%. Doubtful: unsecured part 100% + secured part 20% (≤1 yr), 30% (1–3 yrs), 50% (>3 yrs). Loss 100%. Standard: Base 0.25%; Middle 0.40% (incl. microfinance); Upper: housing/SME 0.25%, teaser 2%, CRE-RH 0.75%, CRE 1%, others 0.40%; extra 5% on each restructuring.
CapitalGold-loan NBFC (≥50% gold loans): Tier 1 12%, CRAR 15%. Middle and above: Tier 1 10%, CRAR 15%. Upper: + CET1 9%. Base: no CRAR — leverage (outside liabilities ÷ owned fund) ≤ 7.
Risk weightsCash, Govt securities, staff loans 0%; PSB bonds 20%; shares, ICDs, secured loans 100%; consumer credit, credit cards 125%. High-quality infra loans: 75% (≥2% repaid) / 50% (≥5% repaid); ROU assets 100%.
Reports by NBFC auditorAdditional report to the Board (Parts A–D: CoR, NOF, deposits, CRAR, prudential norms, NBS-7). Exception report to RBI (Department of Non-Banking Supervision) — only contraventions. Statutory Auditor's Certificate: within 5 working days of signing the audit report, not later than 31 December. CARO 3(xvi) (RBI registration), 3(iii) (loans overdue over 90 days).
Deposits / fraudRating downgraded below investment grade → stop new deposits, report to RBI within 15 working days. Fraud reporting even without intent: cash shortage over ₹10,000, or over ₹5,000 not reported.
NBFC-MFI≥60% of assets in microfinance loans; fails 4 consecutive quarters → remediation plan to RBI.

Event after year endSA 560: up to report date → obtain evidence; after report, before issue → discuss, amend; after issue → new report / prevent reliance
Written representationsSA 580: dated as near as practicable to, but not after, report date
  • s.143(12) fraud: ₹1 crore or more → to Board/Audit Committee within 2 days, reply in 45 days, to Central Government within 15 days; below ₹1 crore → Audit Committee/Board within 2 days. Penalty: ₹5 lakh listed / ₹1 lakh others.
  • IFC (internal financial controls) reporting not for private company with turnover < ₹50 crore and borrowings < ₹25 crore, or OPC / small company.
  • CARO 2020 exempt: banks, insurance, section 8, OPC, small companies, and private companies (not holding/subsidiary of public) meeting all three limits (capital+reserves ≤ ₹1 crore, borrowings ≤ ₹1 crore, revenue ≤ ₹10 crore). Key clauses: 3(ii)(b) working capital > ₹5 crore — quarterly returns; 3(vii) undisputed dues > 6 months; 3(xi) fraud; 3(xvi) RBI registration; 3(xix) liabilities within one year; 3(xx) CSR unspent to Schedule VII fund within 6 months; 3(xxi) only clause for consolidated FS.
  • SEBI resignation (listed): resign within 45 days of quarter end → issue report for that quarter.

5. Quality control (Ch 1)

  • SQC 1 = firm level, all engagements, 6 elements: leadership; ethical requirements; acceptance and continuance; human resources; engagement performance; monitoring. SA 220 = one audit, engagement partner.
  • File assembly within 60 days of audit report · retention at least 7 years · listed audit partner rotation normally within 7 years · independence confirmation at least annually · EQCR (engagement quality control review) completed before report is issued.

6. PSU, internal audit, due diligence, group audit

TopicMust-write
PSU (Ch 15)Government company = 51%+ held by Central/State Government(s) (s.2(45)); C&AG appoints auditors, directs manner of audit (s.143(5)), comments, supplementary audit, test audit. C&AG term 6 years or age 65. Propriety audit = wasteful/avoidable expenditure even if within rules; performance audit = economy, efficiency, effectiveness.
Internal audit (Ch 16)s.138 / Rule 13: listed companies; unlisted public with paid-up capital ₹50 cr+ or turnover ₹200 cr+ or borrowings over ₹100 cr or deposits ₹25 cr+; private with turnover ₹200 cr+ or borrowings over ₹100 cr (preceding year). Comply within 6 months. Use of internal audit by statutory auditor: SA 610.
Due diligence vs investigation vs audit (Ch 17)Audit = opinion on FS, periodic. Investigation = specific purpose, fact-finding, no fixed period, may need 100% check. Due diligence = before a deal (acquisition, loan): financial, tax, legal, commercial checks; projections for 5 years. Forensic = evidence for court (fraud).
Group audit (Ch 13)s.129(3) consolidated FS + Form AOC-1. Subsidiary year-end difference max 6 months (AS) / 3 months (Ind AS). Other auditors' work → Other Matter paragraph. CARO: only 3(xxi).
Answer frame: Provision (SA / section / clause) → facts → analysis → one-line conclusion. Write the RTP figure where red.
