SAAA standards — what each is about + points to write

SA = Standard on Auditing · SRE = Standard on Review Engagements · SAE = Standard on Assurance Engagements · SRS = Standard on Related Services · SQC = Standard on Quality Control · TCWG = those charged with governance · FS = financial statements.

Quality and general principles

StandardWhat it is aboutPoints to write
SQC 1Quality control at firm level, all engagements6 elements: leadership · ethical requirements · acceptance & continuance · human resources · engagement performance · monitoring. File assembly ≤ 60 days; retention ≥ 7 years; listed partner rotation ≤ 7 years; EQCR for listed audits.
SA 220Quality control for one audit, by engagement partnerPartner responsible for overall quality: leadership, ethics/independence, acceptance, team competence, direction-supervision-review, consultation, EQCR before report date.
SA 200Overall objectives of the auditorReasonable assurance FS free from material misstatement (fraud or error); professional skepticism and judgment; inherent limitations — greater for fraud and laws.
SA 210Terms of engagementPreconditions: acceptable framework + management accepts responsibilities; engagement letter; change in terms needs reasonable justification.
SA 230Audit documentationEnough for an experienced auditor to understand work, evidence, conclusions; assembled within 60 days; no deletion after assembly (additions dated, with reasons).
SA 240Auditor's responsibility for fraudTwo types: fraudulent financial reporting, misappropriation of assets. Revenue recognition presumed fraud risk (rebuttable, document). Management override always a risk → test journals, estimates, unusual transactions. Report to management/TCWG; withdraw if needed.
SA 250Laws and regulationsTwo categories: (a) direct effect on FS (tax, pension) → obtain evidence; (b) other laws (licence, environment) → inquiry + inspect correspondence. Non-compliance → TCWG; consider report and regulator.
SA 260Communication with TCWGAuditor's responsibilities; planned scope and timing (incl. significant risks); significant findings (accounting practices, difficulties, written representations); independence (listed). Timely; written for significant findings.
SA 265Communicating internal control deficienciesSignificant deficiencies → in writing to TCWG and management; other deficiencies → management.
SA 299Joint auditDivision of work in writing (communicated to TCWG); each auditor responsible for own area; jointly and severally for common areas (decisions on reporting, non-compliance, presentation). Disagreement → separate report possible.
SA 402Entity uses a service organisationUnderstand services; Type 1 report = design at a date; Type 2 = design + operating effectiveness over a period (needed to rely on controls). Can't refer to service auditor in report unless law requires.

Planning, risk, materiality

StandardWhat it is aboutPoints to write
SA 300PlanningOverall audit strategy (scope, timing, direction) vs audit plan (nature, timing, extent of procedures). Update as needed.
SA 315Understanding entity and risk assessmentRisk assessment procedures: inquiry, analytical procedures, observation and inspection. Internal control components: control environment, risk assessment process, information system, control activities, monitoring. Identify significant risks.
SA 320Materiality in planningOverall materiality + performance materiality (lower, to cover aggregation); revise if new information.
SA 330Responses to assessed risksOverall responses + further procedures (tests of controls, substantive); substantive procedures for every material class regardless of risk.
SA 450Evaluating misstatementsAccumulate (except clearly trivial): factual, judgmental, projected; ask management to correct; uncorrected → assess against materiality; communicate; written representation on uncorrected.
SA 540Accounting estimatesUnderstand method, data, assumptions; test management's process / develop own range / use subsequent events; management bias indicators.
SA 600Using work of another auditorPrincipal auditor assesses other auditor's competence, gives instructions, reviews; reference in report only via Other Matter.
SA 610Using internal auditorsEvaluate objectivity, competence, systematic and disciplined approach. Two ways: use their work, or direct assistance (not on significant judgments, high risk). Auditor alone responsible for opinion.
SA 620Using auditor's expertEvaluate competence, capabilities and objectivity; agree scope in writing; evaluate adequacy of findings. Don't refer in unmodified report. (Management's expert → SA 500.)

Evidence and completion

StandardWhat it is aboutPoints to write
SA 500Audit evidenceSufficient (quantity) and appropriate (relevance + reliability). External, original, documentary, direct evidence is more reliable. Evaluate management's expert (competence, objectivity, work).
SA 501Specific items: inventory, litigation, segmentAttend physical count (if material) — unforeseen: alternate date; impracticable: alternative procedures, else modify. Litigation: inquiry, minutes, legal expenses, letter to entity's lawyer.
SA 505External confirmationsAuditor controls requests. Positive (reply always) vs negative (reply only if disagree — only when low risk, many small homogeneous balances). Management refuses → reasons; unreasonable → TCWG, possible modification.
SA 510Opening balances (initial audit)Read predecessor's working papers / audit current-year procedures; can't obtain evidence → qualified or disclaimer.
SA 520Analytical proceduresSubstantive analytics and near end of audit; investigate unexpected differences.
SA 530Audit samplingStatistical / non-statistical; sampling risk; project misstatements to population.
SA 550Related partiesIdentify relationships; transactions outside normal course = significant risk; check approval, arm's-length claims.
SA 560Subsequent eventsThree periods: to report date (procedures) · after report, before issue (amend, new report) · after issue (new report, prevent reliance).
SA 570Going concernEvaluate management's assessment (12 months from FS date). Outcomes: inappropriate basis → adverse; uncertainty disclosed → "Material Uncertainty" section; not disclosed → qualified/adverse.
SA 580Written representationsOn responsibilities and completeness; dated near to but not after report date; refusal / doubt → disclaimer may be needed.

Reporting

StandardWhat it is aboutPoints to write
SA 700Forming opinion and reportOrder: Opinion → Basis for Opinion → (Going concern / KAM) → Other information → management and TCWG responsibilities → auditor's responsibilities → other legal and regulatory. Signed with membership no., firm reg. no., UDIN, place, date.
SA 701Key audit mattersListed entities (or when chosen/required). From TCWG matters → significant attention → most significance. Not a substitute for modified opinion.
SA 705Modified opinionMaterial not pervasive → qualified; material and pervasive misstatement → adverse; can't get evidence, possible effects material and pervasive → disclaimer.
SA 706Emphasis of Matter / Other MatterEOM = disclosed in FS, fundamental; OMP = not in FS, relevant to audit/report. Neither modifies opinion; not a KAM.
SA 710Comparative informationCorresponding figures (opinion on current only) vs comparative FS (opinion on each year). Prior year unaudited → Other Matter; prior qualification unresolved → modify.
SA 720Other information (annual report)Read and consider for material inconsistency with FS / auditor's knowledge; report section; obtain before report date where possible.
SA 800Special purpose framework FSDescribe the framework; alert that FS may not suit another purpose (and restriction on use if needed).
SA 805Single statement / elementMateriality for that element; if adverse/disclaimer on complete FS, no unmodified opinion on an element that is a major part.
SA 810Summary FSOnly if auditor audited the complete FS; opinion: consistent with / fair summary of audited FS; refers to the audited report.

Review, assurance and related services

StandardWhat it is aboutPoints to write
SRE 2400Review of FS by a practitioner not the auditorLimited assurance; inquiry and analytical procedures; conclusion in negative form ("nothing has come to our attention").
SRE 2410Review of interim FS by the entity's own auditorLimited assurance; uses knowledge from annual audit; negative-form conclusion; modify for departures/scope limits.
SAE 3400Examination of prospective financial informationForecast (best-estimate assumptions) vs projection (hypothetical assumptions). Moderate assurance on assumptions (negative form); opinion on preparation and presentation; caveat that results may differ.
SAE 3402Assurance report on service organisation controlsService auditor's Type 1 / Type 2 report used by user auditors (SA 402).
SAE 3420Pro forma financial information in a prospectusReasonable assurance that compilation is properly done on the stated basis.
SRS 4400Agreed-upon proceduresNo assurance; report of factual findings; restricted to parties who agreed the procedures; independence not required (state if not independent).
SRS 4410Compilation engagementNo assurance; uses accounting expertise to compile; management responsible; report states it is not an audit/review.

Due diligence, investigation, forensic (Ch 17)

  • Due diligence: independent appraisal before acquisition/merger/loan — financial (quality of earnings, working capital, contingent liabilities), tax, legal, commercial, HR, IT. Review 5 years' results; projections on optimistic/pessimistic/likely bases.
  • Investigation: fact-finding for a specific purpose (fraud, share valuation, incoming partner, loan); scope set by appointing authority; no fixed periodicity; may need 100% check; no futuristic opinion.
  • Forensic accounting: gather and present evidence for legal proceedings; fraud examination (s.447 Companies Act, SA 240).

Digital audit (Ch 12) and ESG (Ch 18)

  • IT general controls (access, change management, operations/backup, development) vs application (automated) controls (input, processing, output). CAATs = computer-assisted audit techniques (data analytics on complete populations). Cyber risks: phishing, whaling (senior executives), ransomware, DoS.
  • ESG = environmental, social, governance reporting. BRSR (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report) — 9 principles; assurance under SSAE 3000 / SAE 3410 as per SM; statutory auditor reads it as other information (SA 720).

PSU (Ch 15) and internal audit (Ch 16) — one line each

  • Propriety audit = whether spending was wise/necessary even if within rules. Performance audit = economy, efficiency, effectiveness against objectives. Comprehensive audit of PSUs covers both.
  • Internal audit: s.138 thresholds (see last-day sheet); internal auditor may be CA/CMA/other professional, employee or not; SIAs (Standards on Internal Audit) guide planning, reporting, follow-up (Action Taken Report to Audit Committee).
4-mark theory tip: name the standard + its purpose in one line → 4–5 points from the table → one-line conclusion on what the auditor does.
