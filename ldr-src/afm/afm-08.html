Ch 8 — Mutual Funds

Tested every attempt: NAV working, holding-period return (payout vs reinvest), dividend vs bonus plans, back-solving issue price, closed-end premium / discount.

1Set-up & structure

  • MF = trust pooling savings via units; invests in capital & money market; income & gains shared in proportion to units
  • Four constituents: Sponsor → Trust (trustees) → AMC → Custodian, all SEBI-registered. Trust deed registered under the Registration Act, 1908
  • Sponsor: 5 years in financial services, holds ≥ 40% of AMC net worth. AMC net worth ₹100 crore (SM). Trustees ⅔ independent; AMC board 50% independent. AMC removed by majority of trustees or 75% of unit holders
  • Custodian: SEBI-registered, independent of sponsor & AMC (DP for demat)

3ETF vs index fund; hedge fund vs MF

ETFListed; trades all day at real-time prices close to NAV (open-ended fund deals once a day at NAV); authorised participants act as market makers; low cost, tax-efficient, no paperwork. Types: index, commodity (gold), bond, currency
Hedge fund vs MFLightly regulated private vehicle for selected clients; little public disclosure; performance fee (losses borne by investors); uses leverage, long / short, derivatives

High-water mark fee (SM: ANP Plan): ₹20 cr; 0.10% fixed + 2% above ₹21 cr. Return 29% → value 25.8 cr → fee ₹2 L + 2% × 4.8 cr = ₹11.60 L; return 4.5% → 20.9 cr < 21 → ₹2 L only. Trap: fee on excess over HWM, not on the year's gain.

Advantages

Professional management · diversification · convenient administration · higher returns · low cost (max 2.50% for equity funds; excess borne by AMC) · liquidity · transparency · other benefits (SIP / SWP / switch) · highly regulated · economies of scale · flexibility · convenience

6Return formulas

HPR = (NAV1 − NAV0 + income distributed + capital-gain distributed) ÷ NAV0

Reinvestment: extra units = distribution ÷ reinvestment NAV; return = (total units × closing NAV − cost) ÷ cost

Effective yield p.a. = [(units × closing NAV − investment) + dividends] ÷ investment × 365 ÷ days

Annualise monthly return × 12 (simple) unless told to compound; real return = (1 + nominal) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1

7Dividend vs bonus vs growth plans

  • Build a units table per plan to redemption; value = units × closing NAV; deduct STT / tax on short-term units only; annual return = gain ÷ investment × 12 ÷ months
  • Back-solve issue price (Jan 2026 Q1(a)): value now = investment × (1 + yield × years); units now = value ÷ NAV; undo bonuses latest first (× b ÷ (a + b)); issue price = investment ÷ original units. SM: ₹16 L, 17.5% × 5 → ₹30 L ÷ ₹100 = 30,000 → 24,000 → 20,000 → ₹80

8NAV statement (new fund, one month)

  1. Cash = amount raised − underwriting commission − marketing / issue costs − investments bought + sale proceeds + dividends received − expenses paid
  2. Net assets = cash + market value of holdings + receivables (uncollected dividend) − arrears / outstanding expenses
  3. Distribution = % of realised earnings only (sale gains + dividends), deducted from cash
  4. NAV per unit = net assets ÷ units (underwritten issue → all units outstanding)

Only expenses paid leave cash; arrears are deducted from net assets. Units issued on a cheque = cheque ÷ unrounded current NAV; NAV unchanged that day.

11Closed-ended fund premium / discount

Market price = NAV × (1 ± premium / discount). Investor's return uses market prices, so it beats NAV growth if the discount narrows. PYQ May 2026: geometric mean return 3.771% vs NAV growth 2.76%. Geometric mean = [(1 + r1)(1 + r2)…]1/n − 1.

Ch 8 · Fund manager, FIIs, formula plans, SIPTHEORY + NUMERICAL

12Role of fund manager & FIIs

  • Active fund: outperform, generate positive alpha via research & stock picking. Passive: match index with minimum tracking error
  • Other roles (C-M-W-O): Compliances (SEBI / AMFI, reporting), constant Monitoring of performance, Wealth creation & protection, control over Outsourced work
  • FIIs buy units directly from the issuer or through a registered broker on a recognised exchange within SEBI limits; benefits: better corporate governance, more competition, capital inflow (jobs & forex)
  • Fund hedge with index futures: contracts = β × equity value ÷ (futures × lot) — cash not hedged (₹90 cr, β 1.1, 4,300 × 50 → 4,605)

13Formula plans — constant ratio plan

  • Total value = bonds + units × NAV; when NAV move hits the trigger, reset to the fixed ratio — buy units after a fall, sell after a rise
  • ICAI checks each NAV against the previous one (32 → 38 = +18.75% triggers)
  • Wins in choppy markets ending near the start (sells high, buys low); loses in a strong one-way rise (keeps selling the winner)

14SIP / STP / SWP & vocabulary

  • SIP: fixed amount in; average cost = total ₹ ÷ total units = harmonic mean of NAVs ≤ average NAV (₹10,000 at 20, 16, 25, 20 → ₹19.75 vs ₹20.25)
  • SWP: fixed amount out · STP: fixed amount moved scheme to scheme
  • NFO · AUM · folio · cut-off time (before → that day's NAV; after → next day's)
  • Gilt fund: rates expected to fall → lengthen duration; to rise → shorten
