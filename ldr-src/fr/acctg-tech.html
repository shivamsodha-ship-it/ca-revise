2Cloud computing (PYQ May 2026 — 5 marks)

Definition: delivery of computing services over the internet, letting accountants access data & software from any device with internet — kept accounting & reporting running during lockdown.

  • Cloud storage — Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive
  • SaaS — Salesforce (CRM), Slack, QuickBooks Online
  • IaaS — AWS, Azure, Google Cloud (pay-as-you-go servers)
  • PaaS — Azure App Service, Google App Engine
  • Communication & collaboration — Teams, Workspace, Zoom
  • Cloud e-commerce — online stores, payments, inventory
  • Big data analytics — Redshift, BigQuery, Azure Data Lake
  • Content streaming — e.g., Netflix

Benefits: reduced costs (no hardware), scalability, anywhere access, real-time collaboration, improved reporting & analytics, automatic backup / updates.

Threats

5Blockchain & AI in reporting

  • Blockchain: distributed, tamper-evident ledger → transparency, immutability, real-time reporting, fewer reconciliations (shared ledger with counterparties), smart contracts auto-executing terms
  • AI: automated data capture (invoices), anomaly / fraud detection, predictive forecasting, chatbots, continuous audit
  • Automation / RPA: rule-based repetitive tasks — bank recs, intercompany matching, closing entries; facilitates compliance

6Law link — audit trail

  • Companies (Accounts) Rules: accounting software must have an audit trail (edit log) that cannot be disabled (from 1 Apr 2023); auditor reports on it under Rule 11(g) of Audit & Auditors Rules
  • Electronic books: backup on servers physically located in India, on a daily basis
