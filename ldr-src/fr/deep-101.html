4Presentation of first Ind AS FS

  • Three balance sheets: end of current year, end of comparative year, and transition date (opening Ind AS BS)
  • Two P&Ls, two cash flow statements, two SOCEs + notes, incl. Ind AS 101 reconciliations
  • Explain material differences in cash flows (e.g., interest classification)
Draw the line: Transition date → Comparative year → First reporting date. Then go item by item: recognise / derecognise / reclassify / remeasure — check exception or exemption for each.

"As per para 11 of Ind AS 101, the adjustments arising from changes in accounting policies on transition to Ind AS shall be recognised directly in retained earnings (or, if appropriate, another category of equity) at the date of transition to Ind AS."
