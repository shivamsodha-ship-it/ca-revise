4Loan modification — 10% test (borrower)

Loan ₹10,00,000 at 10% (original EIR), 3 years left. Lender agrees to cut interest to 7%; no fees.

Working₹
PV of new cash flows at original EIR 10%: 70,000 × 2.4869 + 10,00,000 × 0.75139,25,394
PV of old remaining cash flows (= carrying amount)10,00,000
Difference 7.46% → < 10% → not substantial
Carrying amount adjusted to 9,25,394; modification gain → P&L74,606

If ≥ 10% (or qualitatively substantial, e.g., currency change, equity conversion feature added) → extinguish old loan, recognise new at FV; difference → P&L; fees → part of gain / loss. Fees in the test: fees paid net of fees received.

6Financial guarantee for subsidiary's bank loan

  • Parent (issuer): guarantee at FV (e.g., fee a bank would charge, say ₹50,000) → Dr investment in sub, Cr financial guarantee liability; amortise to income over guarantee period
  • Later: higher of (a) ECL allowance and (b) initial amount less cumulative income
  • Sub (holder): guarantee from parent → usually integral to loan, not separately accounted
  • CFS → eliminated
Intra-group "free" benefits are never free: the gap between FV and price is a capital contribution (parent → investment; sub → equity).
