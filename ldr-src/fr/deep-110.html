The elimination unwinds through depreciation — not through sale like inventory. Every year: reverse that year's excess depreciation; carry the rest in opening RE.

6Other intra-group items

  • Loans / interest / management fees → eliminate both sides in full; cash / goods in transit → bring into receiver's books first
  • Intra-group loss on sale → eliminate, but it may evidence impairment — then keep the loss
  • Guarantees by parent for sub's loan → eliminated in CFS (no separate liability)

8Chain holdings

  • P owns 80% of S; S owns 60% of T → T is P's sub (control via S). P's effective interest = 48%; NCI in T = 52% (40% direct + 12% indirect via S's NCI)
  • Indirect NCI (S's 20%) also bears 20% of S's cost of investment in T → deduct that from NCI when computing T's goodwill / NCI
  • Sequence: if S bought T before P bought S → use T's net assets at the date P acquired S. If after → T's date of acquisition by S

  • Goodwill inside the investment — not amortised, not tested separately; excess of share of net FV over cost → income in the period acquired
  • Share of losses beyond carrying amount → apply first to LTI (after Ind AS 109 applied to them); then stop unless legal / constructive obligation; resume only after unrecognised losses are covered
  • Reporting date gap ≤ 3 months; uniform policies; potential voting rights ignored for share computation

11Joint operation numbers (Ind AS 111)

A and B each own 50% of a pipeline under a joint operation; total cost 1,000, revenue 300. A records: asset 500, its share of revenue 150 and expenses — no investment line, no equity method. Acquisition of an interest in a JO that is a business → apply Ind AS 103 principles (goodwill, DT, costs expensed).
