5Customer loyalty points (material right)

Sales ₹1,00,000 earn 10,000 points (₹1 each); expected redemption 80% → SSP of points ₹8,000.

Working₹
Goods: 1,00,000 × 1,00,000 ÷ 1,08,00092,593 (revenue now)
Points: 1,00,000 × 8,000 ÷ 1,08,0007,407 (contract liability)
Year 1: 4,500 points redeemed of 8,000 expected → 7,407 × 4,500 ÷ 8,0004,167 revenue

Change in expected redemption → cumulative catch-up on the liability (revenue = liability × cumulative redeemed ÷ revised total expected).

9Principal vs agent

CaseAnswer

11Contract costs — numbers

  • Sales commission ₹3 L on winning a 3-year service contract → asset, amortise ₹1 L / year (straight-line if service evenly delivered); include anticipated renewals if commission relates to them
  • Legal fees for drafting the bid (incurred whether or not won) → expense
  • Commission on a 1-year contract → may expense (practical expedient)
  • Set-up costs of a data centre for an outsourcing contract (not PPE) → fulfilment cost asset if criteria met

12Warranty — split test

FactResult
Standard 1-year cover required by law; repairs defects onlyAssurance → Ind AS 37 provision
Customer can buy extended warranty separatelyService → separate PO, defer revenue
3-year cover where market norm is 1 yearSplit: 1 yr assurance + 2 yrs service PO

13Other must-know

  • Non-refundable upfront fee (gym joining fee) → advance for future services → recognise over membership (and expected renewals if a material right)
  • Breakage on gift cards → recognise expected breakage in proportion to redemptions, if no significant reversal; else when remote
  • Customer acceptance that is objective (meets agreed specs) → doesn't delay control; subjective → wait
  • Consideration payable to customer (slotting fees ₹5 L to retailer) → reduce revenue when the later of revenue recognised / payment promised
  • Collectability doubtful from the start → no contract; cash received = liability

"Since the customer has a material right in the form of loyalty points, the points constitute a separate performance obligation as per para B40 of Ind AS 115. The transaction price is allocated between the goods and the points on the basis of relative stand-alone selling prices, and the amount allocated to the points is recognised as a contract liability until the points are redeemed or expire."
