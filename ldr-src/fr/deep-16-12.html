4Exchange & deferred payment

  • Give machine (CA 300, FV 350) + cash 50 for a new machine (commercial substance) → cost 400; gain 50 on old machine → P&L
  • No commercial substance → cost = CA given up 300 + cash 50 = 350; no gain
  • Price 1,000 payable after 1 year vs cash price 900 → cost 900; 100 = interest (unless Ind AS 23 capitalises)
