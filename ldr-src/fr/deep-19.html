3Accumulating compensated absences — classic

100 employees, 5 days' paid sick leave a year; unused leave carried forward one year; leave taken first from current year. At year-end, average unused = 2 days. Expected next year: 92 employees take ≤ 5 days; 8 take 6.5 days each.

WorkingDays
92 employees use current entitlement only → no carried-forward used0
8 employees use 1.5 days of carried-forward each12
Liability = 12 days of sick pay (expected, not the full 200 days unused)
  • Non-vesting accumulating → still a liability (expected use). Vesting → full amount unused
  • Non-accumulating (lapses) → no liability until absence occurs

Ind AS 19 · Plan events, termination, India casesDEEP DIVE 3 / 3

5Plan amendment / curtailment / settlement mid-year

  1. Remeasure net DBL immediately before the event using current assumptions (difference → OCI)
  2. Past service cost / settlement gain or loss = change in DBO from the event → P&L
  3. For the rest of the year, compute current service cost & net interest using the updated assumptions and remeasured net DBL (2019 amendment)
  4. Asset ceiling effect after the event → OCI
EventMeaning
CurtailmentSignificant reduction in number of employees covered (plant closure) → past service cost (usually a gain)
SettlementEliminates all further obligation (lump-sum buy-out / annuity purchase) — not normal benefit payments per plan terms
Plan amendmentBenefits introduced / changed (incl. by law)

6Termination benefits — VRS

  • VRS offered 1 Mar, acceptance window to 15 Apr: recognise when entity can't withdraw the offer — for a VRS, when employees accept (or a restriction on withdrawing takes effect)
  • Involuntary plan → when communicated with sufficient detail & unlikely to change
  • Measured per benefit nature: within 12 m → undiscounted; beyond → discounted
