6Presentation & tax

  • Redeemable preference shares (liability) → Borrowings under Schedule III; dividends → finance costs
  • Equity issue costs → reduce equity (net of tax); costs of a failed issue → P&L
  • Liability component in a compound: carrying amount vs tax base (full face) → taxable TD → DTL charged to equity (Ind AS 12 AG / para 23); unwinds through P&L
  • Treasury shares: deduct cost from equity; no P&L on sale / cancellation; disclose number held (Ind AS 1)
