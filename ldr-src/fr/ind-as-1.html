4One-liners that fetch marks

  • Extraordinary items — PROHIBITED 87. Exceptional items may be shown (Sch III).
  • No offsetting unless an Ind AS requires/permits 32. OK: gain/loss on disposal of NCA shown net; expense net of Ind AS 37 reimbursement.
  • Materiality: material items shown separately; immaterial item → no disclosure even if an Ind AS lists it.
  • Change in presentation: reclassify comparatives + disclose nature, amount, reason 41 (impracticable → say why).
  • Material accounting policy info (2023 amendment) — significant. Standardised text that just repeats Ind AS = not material.
  • Departure from Ind AS 19 — only "extremely rare" misleading case, framework must not prohibit; heavy disclosure.
  • Judgements 122 ≠ estimation uncertainty 125 (risk of material adjustment within next year).
  • Capital management disclosure 134–136. Dividends proposed after RD + arrears of cumulative pref. → notes 137.

5The tests 66 · 69

ASSET is current if ANY:

  • realised in normal operating cycle
  • held for trading
  • realised within 12 months
  • cash / CE (unless restricted ≥ 12 m)

LIABILITY is current if ANY:

  • settled in normal operating cycle
  • held for trading
  • due within 12 months
  • no RIGHT at RD to defer ≥ 12 m 69(d)
  • Operating cycle not identifiable → assume 12 months 68 · 70
  • Trade payables, accrued employee cost in operating cycle → current even if due > 12 m
  • Deferred tax → NEVER current 56
  • Counterparty's conversion option classified as equity → ignored for classification 69(d)

📷 Photograph test: the BS is a photo at midnight 31 Mar. A right signed in April is a new photo — can't edit the old one.

Approval date NEVER changes refinancing classification (always current) — it only decides disclosure.

"Exactly 12 months" grace = at least 12 → NC.

8Exam-ready sentences

Refinancing after RD: "As per para 69(d) of Ind AS 1, the right to defer settlement must exist at the end of the reporting period. Since the refinancing agreement was concluded after the reporting period, the loan is classified as current (para 72); the refinancing is a non-adjusting event and is disclosed as per para 76 read with Ind AS 10."

Breach waiver: "As per the Indian carve-out in para 74 of Ind AS 1, since the lender agreed after the reporting period but before approval of the financial statements for issue not to demand payment as a consequence of the breach, the liability shall not be classified as current."

Going concern: "Management has identified material uncertainties that may cast significant doubt on the entity's ability to continue as a going concern; the FS are prepared on a going concern basis and these uncertainties are disclosed (para 25)."
