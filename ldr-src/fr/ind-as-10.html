Ind AS 10 — Events after Reporting Period

One question decides everything: did the condition exist on the reporting date?

1The window

  • Approval date = when Board authorises issue, even if shareholders approve later 5
  • If management issues to a supervisory board first → date management authorises issue to it 6
  • Window includes events after public announcement of profit 7
  • Event after FS approved → not in these FS at all; next year's problem.

3Non-adjusting events 3(b) · 10 · 11 · 22

A new condition arising after RD → no change to numbers; disclose if material.

  • Fall in fair value of investments 11
  • Major business combination / disposal of subsidiary
  • Plan to discontinue an operation announced
  • Major asset purchases, HFS classification, expropriation
  • Fire destroying a major plant
  • Major restructuring announced / commenced
  • Major ordinary share transactions
  • Abnormal changes in asset prices / FX rates
  • Tax rate changes enacted after RD
  • Big commitments / guarantees; litigation from post-RD events
Adjusting events explain the past. Non-adjusting events change the future.

4Look-alike twins — trace the CAUSE, not the date

ADJUSTING (cause existed at RD)NON-ADJUSTING (cause arose after RD)
Customer insolvent in April; was already in difficulty on 31 MarCustomer healthy on 31 Mar; fire in April → insolvent
Inventory sold below cost in April; value already lowGovt ban in April → sold as scrap
Court rules on a case pending at RDNew lawsuit from an April incident
Prior-year fraud found in May → restate (Ind AS 8)Market crash in April cuts investment values
Breach on/before RD, waived before approval (Indian carve-out)Loan refinanced after RD

Para 9(b)(i) says bankruptcy "usually" confirms a year-end loss — usually ≠ always. If the customer was healthy at RD, it is non-adjusting.

Ask: "Was this already true at midnight on 31 March?" Yes → adjust. No → disclose.

5Going concern override 14–16

After RD, management intends to liquidate / cease or has no realistic alternative → do NOT prepare on GC basis.

Only exception to the photo test — it changes the basis, not just a figure. No formal resolution needed.

6Dividends 12–13

Declared after RD → NOT a liability (no obligation at RD) → disclose (Ind AS 1 para 137).

Board recommends final dividend before RD, AGM after → still not a liability. Interim declared by Board before RD → liability.

9Approval-date nuances 5 · 6 · 7

  • Window closes at Board approval — audit report date, AGM.
  • Supervisory board of non-executives → date management authorises issue to it 6
