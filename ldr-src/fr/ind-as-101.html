Ind AS 101 — First-time Adoption

Default = full retrospective application. Then mandatory exceptions (must) and optional exemptions (may) carve it down.

1The dates — draw this first

TermExample: first Ind AS FS for FY 2025-26
Date of transition = start of earliest comparative period1 April 2024 — opening Ind AS balance sheet prepared here
Comparative periodFY 2024-25 — restated under Ind AS
First Ind AS reporting period / reporting dateFY 2025-26 / 31 March 2026
Ind AS versions usedThose effective at 31 March 2026, applied to all periods 7 – 8
  • First Ind AS FS = first FS with an explicit and unreserved statement of compliance with Ind AS 3

2Opening Ind AS balance sheet 10 – 11

  • Recognise all assets / liabilities Ind AS requires
  • Derecognise items Ind AS doesn't permit
  • Reclassify items (e.g., redeemable pref. shares → liability)
  • Measure per Ind AS

All adjustments → retained earnings (or another equity category) at the date of transition — not P&L of any period.

Previous-GAAP proposed dividend liability → reversed (not a liability under Ind AS 10).

ExemptionWhat it allows
Business combinations CDon't restate past BCs. If you restate one, restate all later ones. Goodwill carried forward; test for impairment at transition

Compound instrumentsNo split if liability component no longer outstanding at transition D18

Exception vs exemption: exceptions (App B) are compulsory — no hindsight on estimates, prospective derecognition. Exemptions (App C / D) are elective, item by item (some all-or-nothing, e.g., D7AA "all PPE").

Exception = Compulsory. Exemption = Elective.

5Reconciliations to disclose 23 – 33

  • Equity: previous GAAP → Ind AS at (a) transition date and (b) end of latest previous-GAAP period
  • Total comprehensive income for the latest previous-GAAP period (start from previous-GAAP profit)
  • Material adjustments to cash flow statement explained
  • Errors under previous GAAP shown separately from policy changes 26
  • Impairment losses recognised / reversed at transition (Ind AS 36 disclosures)
  • Use of FV as deemed cost → aggregate FV and adjustment to carrying amounts, per line item 30
  • Interim reports within first Ind AS year → same reconciliations 32

6Indian roadmap points

  • Companies (Ind AS) Rules, 2015 — mandatory by phases (listed / net-worth thresholds); holding, subsidiary, JV, associate of a covered company also apply
  • Voluntary adoption allowed; once on Ind AS, cannot revert to AS
  • NBFCs, banks, insurers — separate RBI / IRDAI roadmaps

7Cross-links

  • Ind AS 8 doesn't apply to policy changes on first-time adoption
  • Ind AS 1 · 40A — third balance sheet: the transition-date BS is presented
  • Ind AS 12 — DT on all transition adjustments; most go to retained earnings
  • Ind AS 103 App C / 110 — consolidation of subs already acquired: carrying amounts per exemption
