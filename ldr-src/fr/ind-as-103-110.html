Ind AS 103 & 110 — Consolidation

103 = Day 1, the purchase (goodwill, NCI). 110 = every day after (consolidate, stake changes, loss of control).

1Is it a business combination? 103 · 3 · B7

  • Business = inputs + a substantive process that together can contribute to outputs. Optional concentration test: substantially all FV in one asset (or group of similar assets) → not a business.
  • Not a business → asset acquisition: spread cost by relative FV · no goodwill · no deferred tax on initial recognition.
  • Acquirer = whoever gets control (Ind AS 110). Reverse acquisition: the company that legally issues shares is the accounting acquiree.
  • Acquisition date = date control passes. agreement date payment date

2Goodwill 103 · 32

Goodwill = Consideration + NCI + FV of earlier stake − Net identifiable assets (all at FV, on acquisition date)

Negative → recheck all figures → still negative = bargain purchase: clear reason → OCI → capital reserve; no clear reason → directly to capital reserve. Never P&L. 34–36

Ind AS 103 · Fair values, step acquisition, common controlTHEORY MARKS

4Identifiable assets & liabilities 10–31 · 45

  • All at FV — including intangibles S never recorded (brand, customer list) if separable or contractual.
  • Contingent liability: recognise if present obligation + FV reliable, even if outflow not probable 23 (unlike Ind AS 37).
  • Use their own standard: deferred tax (12), employee benefits (19), held for sale (105, FV less cost to sell), leases (116), share-based payment (102), indemnification assets, reacquired rights.
  • Measurement period ≤ 12 months: new info about Day-1 facts → adjust provisional figures and goodwill retrospectively.

5Step acquisition 103 · 41–42

  • Held a stake, then buy more and get control → acquisition date = control date.
  • Remeasure old stake at FV on control date; gain/loss → P&L (FVOCI stake: stays in OCI, may move within equity).
  • That FV goes into the goodwill formula.

SM Illustration 5: 30% associate (carrying ₹100 lakh, FV ₹300 lakh) + buy 70% for ₹700 lakh; net assets ₹800 lakh → Goodwill = 700 + 300 − 800 = 200 · gain on old stake 300 − 100 = 200 to P&L.

Ind AS 110 · Control · ExemptionsTHEORY MARKS

7Control = Power + Returns + Link 110 · 7 · B2

All three needed: power over relevant activities + exposure to variable returns + ability to use the power to affect those returns.
PointRule
Relevant activitiesActivities that significantly affect returns. Different investors direct different ones → the one directing the most significant has power.
Substantive vs protective rightsOnly substantive rights (practical ability to exercise) count. Protective rights (veto on fundamental changes) never give power.
Potential voting rightsCount only if substantive (usually exercisable now, not deep out of the money).
Less than 50% votesDe facto control possible: large holding vs many small, dispersed holders; voting patterns.
Agent vs principalA decision-maker acting as agent doesn't control.
ReassessWhenever facts show a change in any of the three elements.

8Who need not consolidate 110 · 4 · 31

  • Exempt only if all four: (i) S of another entity, owners informed & don't object · (ii) not listed · (iii) not filing to list · (iv) its parent publishes Ind AS consolidated FS.
  • Investment entity: measures subsidiaries at FVTPL, except subsidiaries giving investment-related services.
  • Post-employment benefit plans (gratuity trust) → Ind AS 19, not consolidated.

"As per para 7 of Ind AS 110, an investor controls an investee if and only if it has power over the investee, exposure or rights to variable returns from its involvement, and the ability to use its power to affect the amount of its returns. Since all three elements are present / absent, X Ltd. does / does not control Y Ltd."

10Splitting S's reserves — the passbook

Write S's RE like a bank passbook: events in date order with a running balance.

  1. Profit earned in the year = Closing − Opening + Dividend paid (dividend went out, so put it back).
  2. Spread it evenly over the months (unless told it's seasonal).
  3. Put each dividend on its date. The running balance must end at the closing RE ✓.
  4. Pre-acq = balance on acquisition date. Post-acq = Closing − Pre-acq.

20Loss of control — step by step B98–B99

  1. Remove S's assets, liabilities and goodwill; remove NCI.
  2. Bring in cash received and the stake kept at FV (= cost of associate / Ind AS 109 asset).
  3. OCI: P's share of FCTR → P&L; revaluation surplus → RE directly. NCI's FCTR leaves with NCI.
  4. Difference → P&L. Disclose the part from remeasuring the stake kept.
