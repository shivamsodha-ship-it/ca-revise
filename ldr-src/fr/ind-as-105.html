Ind AS 105 — Held for Sale & Discontinued Ops

Classify (strict criteria) → measure at lower of CA and FV − CTS → stop depreciation → present separately.

1HFS criteria — ALL must be met at RD 6 – 8

Available for immediate sale

In present condition, on usual / customary terms (e.g., not still under construction, not needing major repair the buyer demands)

Sale highly probable

  • Management (appropriate level) committed to a plan
  • Active programme to locate a buyer started
  • Actively marketed at a reasonable price vs FV
  • Completion expected within 1 year
  • Significant changes / withdrawal unlikely
  • Shareholder approval probability considered
  • > 1 year allowed if delay due to events beyond control and entity remains committed 9 · App B
  • Asset acquired exclusively for resale → HFS at acquisition if 1-year test met and others met within a short period (≈ 3 months) 11
  • Criteria met after RD (before approval) → not HFS at RD; disclose (Ind AS 10 non-adjusting) 12
  • Abandoned assets → not HFS (recovered through use) — but may be a discontinued operation when abandoned 13
  • Held for distribution to owners → same treatment (FV less costs to distribute) 12A

2Measurement 15 – 25

  • Just before classification → measure under applicable Ind AS (depreciate, impair)
  • Then: lower of carrying amount and FV less costs to sell
  • Write-down → impairment loss in P&L; later increase in FV − CTS → gain but only up to cumulative losses recognised (Ind AS 105 + earlier Ind AS 36)
  • No depreciation / amortisation while HFS; interest & other expenses of liabilities in a disposal group continue
  • Sale beyond 1 year → costs to sell discounted; unwinding → finance cost

3Disposal groups

  • Group of assets (+ directly associated liabilities) disposed together in one transaction
  • Impairment loss allocated to non-current assets in scope in Ind AS 36 order: goodwill first, then pro rata 23
  • Measurement-scope exclusions (keep own standard): DTA, employee-benefit assets, Ind AS 109 financial assets, biological assets at FV − CTS, insurance contracts 5
  • Plan to sell a subsidiary with loss of control → classify all its assets & liabilities as HFS, even if a non-controlling stake is retained 8A
  • Investment in associate / JV becoming HFS → stop equity method for that portion (Ind AS 28 · 20)

4Change of plan 26 – 29

No longer HFS → measure at lower of: (a) carrying amount before classification, adjusted for depreciation / amortisation / revaluation that would have been recognised; and (b) recoverable amount at the date of decision. Adjustment → P&L from continuing operations.

5Discontinued operation 32

A component disposed of / HFS that: is a separate major line of business or geographical area; or part of a single plan to dispose of one; or a subsidiary acquired exclusively for resale.
