Ind AS 109 — Financial Instruments

Classify (business model + SPPI) → measure → impair (ECL) → derecognise → hedge.

1Financial assets — classification 4.1

Business modelSPPI?Category

Hold to collect & sellYesFVOCI (debt) — recycled
Other (trading, FV-managed)—FVTPL
AnyNo (e.g., equity, convertible, leveraged)FVTPL
  • Equity not held for trading (and not BC contingent consideration) → irrevocable FVOCI election at initial recognition; never recycled; dividends → P&L 5.7.5
  • Fair value option → designate FVTPL if it eliminates an accounting mismatch (irrevocable)
  • Sales under HTC are fine if infrequent / insignificant or due to credit deterioration

Reclassification of financial assets only when the business model changes (rare, significant, demonstrable) → prospective from the first day of the next reporting period. Never for FVOCI equity, FVO designations, or financial liabilities.

2Financial liabilities 4.2

  • Default: amortised cost (EIR). FVTPL if held for trading, derivatives, or designated
  • Designated FVTPL → change from own credit risk → OCI (never recycled), rest P&L — unless OCI creates a mismatch 5.7.7
  • Financial guarantee (issuer) → initially FV; later higher of ECL allowance and initial amount less cumulative income

3Initial measurement 5.1

  • FV + transaction costs (except FVTPL → costs to P&L). Trade receivables without significant financing → transaction price
  • Day-1 difference (FV ≠ price): recognise in P&L only if FV is Level 1 or uses only observable inputs; else defer B5.1.2A
  • Interest-free loan to employee / sub → FV (PV at market rate); difference = employee benefit / investment in sub (parent SFS)

4Expected credit loss — general (3-stage) approach 5.5

StageTriggerAllowanceInterest on
1No significant increase in credit risk (SICR)12-month ECLGross carrying amount
2SICR since initial recognitionLifetime ECLGross carrying amount
3Credit-impairedLifetime ECLNet (amortised cost)
  • Rebuttable presumptions: > 30 days past due = SICR; > 90 days = default
  • Simplified approach (always lifetime ECL; provision matrix) — mandatory for trade receivables / contract assets without significant financing; policy choice for those with financing and lease receivables
  • POCI (purchased / originated credit-impaired) → credit-adjusted EIR; recognise only changes in lifetime ECL
  • FVOCI debt → ECL allowance in OCI, not reducing carrying amount (which stays at FV)
  • ECL = probability-weighted, time value, reasonable & supportable info incl. forward-looking

5Modification & derecognition of liabilities 3.3

  • Liability derecognised when extinguished (discharged, cancelled, expired)
  • 10% test: PV of new cash flows (incl. fees, discounted at original EIR) differs by ≥ 10% from PV of remaining old cash flows → substantial → extinguish old, recognise new at FV; difference → P&L
  • Not substantial → adjust carrying amount; costs amortised over remaining term
  • Asset modification not causing derecognition → recalculate gross carrying amount at original EIR; gain / loss → P&L 5.4.3
ECL stages move both ways — improvement takes Stage 2 back to Stage 1 (12-month ECL).

6Derecognition of financial assets — decision tree 3.2

  1. Consolidate subsidiaries / SPVs first
  2. Rights to cash flows expired? → derecognise
  3. Rights transferred, or pass-through met (no obligation unless collected; can't sell / pledge; remit without material delay)? If neither → keep
  4. Substantially all risks & rewards transferred → derecognise; retained → keep (proceeds = collateralised borrowing)
  5. Neither transferred nor retained → control retained? No → derecognise; Yes → continuing involvement
ExampleResult
Factoring without recourseDerecognise
Factoring with full recourse / guarantee of all lossesKeep + liability
Sale + repurchase at fixed price + lender's return (repo)Keep — financing
Sale + call option deeply out of the moneyDerecognise

7Derivatives & embedded derivatives

  • Derivative: value changes with an underlying, little / no initial investment, settled in future → FVTPL
  • Hybrid with a financial-asset host in Ind AS 109 → no separation; classify whole (usually fails SPPI → FVTPL)
  • Other hosts (liability, non-financial) → separate if not closely related, would meet derivative definition, hybrid not at FVTPL
  • Own-use contracts for non-financial items (normal purchase / sale) → outside 109 unless net settled
