3Principal vs agent B58 – B72

  • A decision-maker acting as agent for others does not control. Weigh: scope of authority, kick-out rights held by others, remuneration (market-based?), other interests (exposure to variability)

4Who need NOT prepare CFS 4(a)

  • Wholly-owned sub, or partly-owned with other owners informed & not objecting; debt/equity not publicly traded; not filing to issue; ultimate / intermediate parent publishes Ind AS CFS

  • Law: Sec 129(3) requires CFS; exemption under Rule 6, Companies (Accounts) Rules on similar conditions

6Goodwill & NCI measurement Ind AS 103

  • NCI choice per acquisition: FV (full goodwill) or proportionate share of identifiable net assets (partial goodwill) — only for present ownership instruments with proportionate share on liquidation
  • Bargain purchase → reassess first; then gain in OCI → capital reserve if clear evidence of reasons; otherwise directly to capital reserve (Indian change; IAS 103 → P&L)
  • Goodwill impairment under full goodwill → shared parent / NCI in profit-sharing ratio; partial → gross-up for testing, only parent share recognised

7Procedures B86 – B95

  • Uniform policies — adjust sub's FS B87
  • Reporting dates differ → sub prepares extra FS; if impracticable, gap ≤ 3 months + adjust significant events B92 – B93
  • Intra-group balances, transactions, URP eliminated in full; losses may signal impairment B86(c)
  • Profit / OCI attributed to NCI even if NCI goes into deficit B94
  • Cumulative preference shares held by NCI → parent's share computed after those dividends, declared or not B95 (LDR Topic 1)

10Loss of control — gain computation

FV of consideration received + FV of retained interest + carrying amount of NCI
− net assets of sub (incl. goodwill) at carrying amount
= gain / loss → P&L (attributable to parent)  ± OCI recycled (FCTR, CFH) / RE transfer (revaluation surplus)

Linked transactions (B97): two steps that are really one deal (same time, in contemplation of each other, one depends on the other, uneconomic alone) → treat as a single loss-of-control transaction. Stops "sell 15% to equity, then 50% to P&L" games.

11Policy vs estimate inside consolidation

  • Sub uses different policy (FIFO vs weighted average) → align to group policy in CFS
  • Sub uses different estimate (useful life) based on its own conditions → no adjustment needed
While control lasts, the owners swap among themselves → equity. When control is lost, the group sells a business → P&L.

13Associates & JVs — Ind AS 28 quick hits

  • Significant influence presumed at ≥ 20% (rebuttable); indicators: board seat, policy participation, material transactions, interchange of managers, technical info
  • Equity method: cost + share of post-acq profit / OCI − dividends received
  • Losses beyond interest → stop at zero unless legal / constructive obligation; long-term interests (LTI) absorb losses after Ind AS 109 is applied to them first
  • Up / downstream URP with associate → eliminated only to the investor's share (unlike subsidiary — full)
  • Impairment → whole investment tested under Ind AS 36 (goodwill not separately)
