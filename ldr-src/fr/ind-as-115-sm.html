Ind AS 115 — Revenue from Contracts with Customers

Short notes from ICAI SM Chapter 9 + RTP / MTP / past papers. Pattern: every paper = one 5-mark Q5(a) + 1–2 MCQs. Asked: contract modification (Sep 2025, May 2026 MCQ, May 2023), significant financing (Jan 2026, Nov 2022), bill-and-hold (May 2026, May 2022), loyalty points / material right (Nov 2024, May 2024, Nov 2022), licences (May 2025), right of return + volume rebates (May 2022), uninstalled materials (May 2023), service concession (Nov 2022), scope MCQ (Jan 2026).

1The five steps and scope 115.2–9

StepQuestion to answer
1 ContractIs there an enforceable contract meeting all 5 criteria? Combine? Modified?
2 Performance obligationsWhich promises are distinct? (each distinct good/service or series = one PO)
3 Transaction priceFixed + variable (constrained) − financing effect + non-cash FV − consideration payable to customer
4 AllocateTo POs on relative stand-alone selling prices (discount and variable amounts may go to specific POs)
5 RecogniseWhen (or as) control passes — over time (3 tests) or at a point in time
  • Out of scope: leases (116), insurance (117), financial instruments (109), and non-monetary exchanges between entities in the same line of business to facilitate sales (two oil companies swapping oil; Jan 2026 MCQ: same-industry bottle swap).
  • Barter between different businesses is in scope — measure non-cash consideration at FV (TYK 2: talk time for power — each records revenue ₹ 50,000 at the FV of what it receives).
  • Ind AS 115 vs AS 9/7: control-based, 5 steps, financing adjusted, contract costs capitalised, detailed disclosures.

2Step 1 — the contract 115.9–21

All 5 criteria: (1) approved and parties committed · (2) each party's rights identifiable · (3) payment terms identifiable · (4) commercial substance · (5) collection of the consideration probable (ability and intention to pay; the amount may be after expected price concessions — TYK 5: price ₹ 2 m, expected 1.75 m → transaction price 1.75 m and the contract exists).

Criteria not met (TYK 4: P pays 5% non-refundable deposit, credit risk high): cash received = liability until (a) no obligations remain and substantially all consideration received and non-refundable, or (b) contract terminated and cash non-refundable. Keep reassessing.

  • Contract term = period of enforceable rights. Gym, 12 months but cancellable free after 3 → term 3 months (Illus 2). 100 cameras but cancellable free after 10 → contract for 10 units + option for 90 (Illus 3).
  • Combine contracts (same customer/related parties, about the same time) if: negotiated as one package with one commercial objective, OR price of one depends on the other, OR goods form a single PO (Illus 5: licence + customisation 3 days later → combine; Illus 6: two government entities, discount only if both buy → combine; Illus 4: planes + spare parts → separate).

3Step 2 — performance obligations 115.22–30

Distinct = both: (a) capable — customer can benefit on its own or with readily available resources; (b) separately identifiable in the contract. Not separately identifiable if: significant integration into a combined output, one significantly modifies/customises another, or they are highly interdependent.

Series of substantially the same goods/services with the same pattern of transfer = one PO (cable TV each month — Illus 24; hotel management each day — Illus 13; 2,000 customised engines transferred over time — RTP May 2026).

Customer options, loyalty points, material rights B39–B43

  • Option that gives a material right (a discount the customer would not get without the contract) = separate PO → allocate price on relative SSP; revenue when used or lapses.
  • SSP of an option = discount × expected purchase × likelihood of use, excluding any discount everyone gets anyway.

Illus 23 (Nov 2022, May 2024 variants): 40% voucher, but 10% to all → incremental 30% × ₹ 500 average purchase × 80% = SSP 120; allocate 1,000 × 1,000/1,120 = 890 product, 110 voucher. PQ 9: 1 point per ₹ 100, point worth ₹ 5, 100 points × 5 = 500 → points 10,000 × 500/10,500 = 476 deferred, 9,524 now. Illus 22 (renewal option): 7,500 + 6,000 + 6,000 with 50% attrition → 12,00,000 / 175 memberships = 6,857 revenue per member in year 1, 643 contract liability. Loyalty points (PQ 5; Nov 2024): allocate sales between goods and points on relative SSP (points × value × expected redemption); release points revenue = allocated amount × points redeemed ÷ total points expected to be redeemed; re-estimate each year, catch up.

Principal vs agent · consignment · upfront fees B34–B38, B77–B78

  • Principal = controls the good/service before transfer (indicators: primarily responsible, inventory risk, price discretion) → revenue gross. Agent → net commission. Illus 26: ticket reseller bound to buy tickets → principal. Illus 27: ad agency controls the service though no inventory risk/no price discretion → principal.
  • Consignment: no revenue on delivery to the consignee; revenue when the consignee sells or must pay (Illus 25).
  • Non-refundable upfront fee not tied to a transferred good → advance for future goods; recognise over the period (include renewals if it gives a material right).

4Step 3 — transaction price 115.47–72

Variable consideration and the constraint

  • Discounts, rebates, refunds, credits, price concessions, incentives, bonuses, penalties → estimate by expected value (many contracts / range of outcomes) or most likely amount (two outcomes), whichever predicts better; estimate each element separately.
  • Constraint: include only to the extent it is highly probable that a significant reversal will not occur. Re-assess every reporting date; changes go to revenue (catch-up).
  • Cash discount for early payment (RTP May 2022): if paid after year-end but before approval, it is evidence → adjust sales.

Illus 30: ₹ 25 cr + early-completion bonus (expected value 2.125 cr) + quality bonus (most likely 2 cr) = 29.125 cr. PQ 3: 1,30,000 (90%) or 1,10,000 → most likely 1,30,000. PQ 2 (price protection): 5,000 × 70% + 4,500 × 20% + 4,000 × 10% = 4,800 per TV. PQ 1: 1,000 − expected reimbursement 50 = 950.

Volume rebates: Illus 31 (HT Ltd) — Q1: 10 units × 1,000 = 10,000 (100 units not expected); Q2: estimate changes → 50 × 900 − 10 × 100 = 44,000. PQ 10 (chargers, same logic): 53,050. TYK 6: expected 2.8 m containers → price ₹ 90; Q1 7 lakh containers → revenue 63 m, refund liability 7 m. May 2022 PYQ (nut bolts): liability 24,000 × (200 − 190) = 2,40,000.

Sale with right of return B20–B27

  • Revenue only for goods not expected to be returned; refund liability for the rest; asset (right to recover goods) at former carrying amount less recovery costs and value loss; reduce cost of sales.

Illus 34: 1,000 × ₹ 50, 3% expected back, cost ₹ 30 → revenue 48,500; refund liability 1,500; asset 900; cost of sales 29,100. (MTP Nov 2026: 10,000 moulds, same steps; May 2022 PYQ car locks.)

Significant financing component 115.60–65

When: payment timing gives a significant financing benefit (gap between cash price and promised amount; long interval; interest rates). Not significant if: advance paid and timing at customer's discretion; consideration variable on an uncontrollable event (sales royalty — Illus 44); difference is for another reason (retention money protecting the customer — Illus 41; advance from low-credit customers — Illus 43). Practical expedient: gap ≤ 1 year → ignore (Illus 47).

How: transaction price = PV at the rate for a separate financing transaction with that customer (credit-adjusted), not the contract rate if that is lower. Payment in arrears → revenue at PV, interest income (Ind AS 109). Advance received → contract liability grows with interest expense; revenue = liability at transfer.

Illus 38: ₹ 1,21,000 after 2 years; cash price 1,00,000 → revenue 1,00,000, interest 21,000 over 2 years. Illus 39 Case B: 60 × 2,12,470 at 14% → TP 91,31,346. Illus 40: advance 4,000, 6% for 2 years → interest expense 494; revenue 4,494. Jan 2026 Q5(a): 5 × 2,50,438 at 8% contract, 10% market → TP = 2,50,438 × 3.79 = 9,49,160. Mercury / Mohan (MTPs, Nov 2022): instalments discounted → sale at PV (Mercury 9,50,000), finance income over 2 years.

Non-cash consideration · consideration payable to a customer

  • Non-cash → FV at contract inception (later changes due to its form are not revenue). If FV not reliable → SSP of goods given. Illus 50: 1,000 + ad slots FV 600 = 1,600 (else 1,500). Illus 51: customer-provided tooling FV 2 cr that the supplier controls → TP 27 cr. Illus 49: shares bonus valued at inception ₹ 100 → 360 cr if earned.
  • Payment to customer (slotting fees, coupons, rebates, credits) → reduce revenue unless for a distinct good/service at FV. Recognise the reduction at the later of revenue and promise to pay. Illus 52: 1.5 cr on 15 cr commitment = 10% → first month 1.25 − 0.125 = 1.125 cr. Illus 53: port-in credit → TP 19,200 − 2,000 = 17,200. TYK 7: rebate coupon → 50 − 25% × 10 = 47.50. RTP Jan 2026: upfront ₹ 50,000 → reduce revenue in proportion to sales (Q2: 1,22,500).

5Step 4 — allocate the price 115.73–90

  • SSP: observable price → else adjusted market assessment / expected cost plus margin / residual (only if price highly variable or uncertain). Illus 54: 10,000 on SSPs 5,000 / 2,500 / 7,500 → 3,300 / 1,700 / 5,000.
  • Discount goes proportionately unless all 3: each item sold separately; a bundle of some is regularly sold at a discount; that discount ≈ the contract discount → put it on that bundle (Illus 55A; PQ 7). Residual only if result is within the observable range (Illus 55B/C).
  • Variable consideration goes to one PO if it relates specifically to it and that is consistent with the objective (Illus 56: royalty on Licence B only).
  • Changes in TP after inception: allocate on the inception SSP basis; satisfied POs → revenue now (Illus 57: bonus 20 lakh to implementation → 60% × 1.2 cr = 72 lakh to date). Discretionary retention credit → change in price; recognise over remaining term (Illus 58).

  • Illus 73 (IT outsourcing ₹ 4 lakh): hardware → Ind AS 16; software → Ind AS 38; design and migration/testing → Ind AS 115 asset, amortised over 7 years.

8Presentation · foreign currency advance

  • Contract asset = right to consideration conditional on something other than time (performance done, not yet billable). Receivable = unconditional right (only time). Contract liability = cash received/due before performance.
  • Advance in foreign currency (RTP Sep 2025; MTP Nov 2026): advance is non-monetary → revenue at the rate on the date of the advance for that portion (Ind AS 21 Appendix B); rest at transaction-date rates.
  • Disclose: disaggregated revenue, contract balances, remaining POs, judgements.

10Exam checklist

Modification: distinct + SSP → new contract; distinct, not SSP → prospective; not distinct → cumulative catch-up (new price × new % − revenue to date).
Variable consideration: expected value vs most likely; constrain; volume rebate → refund liability for the price difference.
Financing: > 1 year and significant → discount at the separate-financing rate; arrears → interest income; advance → interest expense on contract liability.
Material right / points: SSP = incremental discount × purchase × likelihood → allocate → defer → release on use.
Right of return: revenue on expected-to-keep; refund liability; asset at cost.
Bill-and-hold: 4 criteria + separate custodial PO over time.
Licence: ongoing activities that change the IP → access (over time); static IP → use (point in time).
Uninstalled materials: revenue = cost, excluded from % complete.
Always write: identify the PO → transaction price → allocation → timing, with the paragraph reference.
