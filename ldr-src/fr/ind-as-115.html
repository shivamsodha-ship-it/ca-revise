2Step 1 — contract traps

3Step 3 — transaction price

  • Variable consideration (discounts, rebates, bonuses, penalties, returns): expected value or most likely amount, then constraint — include only to extent highly probable no significant reversal 53 · 56
  • Significant financing component → adjust for time value; expedient if gap ≤ 1 year 60 – 63
  • Non-cash consideration → FV 66
  • Consideration payable to customer (slotting fees, coupons) → reduce revenue, unless for a distinct good / service at FV 70

4Step 4 — allocation 73 – 86

  • SSP not observable → adjusted market assessment, expected cost + margin, residual (only if highly variable / uncertain)
  • Discount → proportionately to all POs, unless observable evidence it relates to some 81 – 82
  • Variable consideration → wholly to one PO if terms relate to it and result is consistent with objective 85

5Step 5 — over time? (any ONE) 35

(a) Customer simultaneously receives & consumes benefits (cleaning, payroll)

(b) Performance creates / enhances an asset the customer controls as it's built

(c) No alternative use and enforceable right to payment for work done to date

  • Measure progress: output (milestones, units) or input (cost-to-cost) — exclude costs not reflecting progress (wasted materials); uninstalled materials → revenue = cost (zero margin) B19
  • Can't measure progress but expect to recover costs → revenue to extent of costs 45

Point in time — control indicators 38

Present right to payment · legal title · physical possession · significant risks & rewards · customer acceptance

7Contract costs 91 – 104

  • Incremental cost of obtaining (sales commission on winning) → asset if expected recoverable; expedient: expense if amortisation ≤ 1 year
  • Costs incurred regardless of win (bid costs, legal) → expense unless explicitly chargeable
  • Costs to fulfil (not under other Ind AS) → asset if relate to contract, generate resources, expected recovered
  • Amortise consistent with transfer of goods; impairment test

9Indian specifics

  • Reconciliation of revenue recognised with contracted price, showing adjustments (discounts, rebates, refunds, credits, price concessions, incentives, bonuses) 126AA
  • Scope out: leases (116), insurance (117), financial instruments (109), non-monetary exchanges between same-line entities to facilitate sales
  • Interest & dividend income → Ind AS 109, not 115
