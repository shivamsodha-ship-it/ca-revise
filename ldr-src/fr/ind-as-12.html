Ind AS 12 — Income Taxes

Balance sheet approach: compare carrying amount with tax base. Everything else is an exception or a measurement rule.

1The core grid

Carrying amount > Tax baseCarrying amount < Tax base
ASSETTaxable TD → DTLDeductible TD → DTA
LIABILITYDeductible TD → DTATaxable TD → DTL
Asset bigger in books → DTL. Liability bigger in books → DTA.

2Tax base 7 · 8

Asset

Amount deductible for tax against future taxable benefits.

Benefits not taxable (e.g., dividend receivable not taxable) → tax base = carrying amount (no TD).

Liability

Carrying amount − amount deductible in future.

Revenue received in advance: CA − revenue not taxable in future.

3Unused losses & credits 34 – 36

  • DTA for unused tax losses / credits → only to extent probable future taxable profit
  • History of recent losses → need convincing other evidence (taxable TDs, firm contracts, tax planning) 35
  • MAT credit → recognised as DTA when probable it will be utilised within the allowed period (ICAI guidance)

Ind AS 12 · Exceptions & measurementMOST TESTED

4Initial recognition exceptions 15 · 24

No DT is recognised for:

  • DTL on initial recognition of goodwill 15(a) (DTA on tax-deductible goodwill excess — allowed)
  • Initial recognition of an asset / liability in a transaction that:
    • is not a business combination, and
    • affects neither accounting profit nor taxable profit, and
    • does not give rise to equal taxable and deductible TDs (2023 amendment)

2023 amendment: leases (ROU asset + lease liability) and decommissioning (asset + provision) create equal and opposite TDs → exception no longer applies → recognise both DTA and DTL (gross; offset only if para 74 met).

5Measurement 46 – 56

  • Rates enacted or substantively enacted by RD 47 — change after RD = Ind AS 10 non-adjusting
  • Rate expected when TD reverses; reflect manner of recovery (use vs sale) 51
  • Revalued non-depreciable asset (land) → rate for recovery through sale 51B
  • No discounting of DTA / DTL 53
  • Review DTA each RD — reduce if not probable; reassess unrecognised DTA too 37 · 56

Sec 115BAA (new regime 22% + surcharge & cess)

Measure DT at the rate expected when TDs reverse: if the company expects to opt in a later year, TDs reversing after that year use the new rate. Remeasurement effect → P&L (or OCI / equity if the item was there).

7Investments in subs, branches, associates, JVs 39 · 44

Recognise unless …
DTLparent / investor controls timing of reversal and it is probable not to reverse in foreseeable future 39
DTArecognise only if probable to reverse in foreseeable future and taxable profit available 44

Associate: investor usually cannot control dividend policy → DTL on undistributed profits generally recognised (unless agreement says profits won't be distributed). Subsidiary: parent controls → often not recognised.

8Consolidation adjustments

  • Unrealised profit in inventory eliminated → deductible TD → DTA at buyer's rate (buyer holds the asset)
  • Downstream → DTA all to parent; upstream → DTA split parent / NCI (see LDR Topic 2)
  • Group DT computed on consolidated carrying amounts vs each entity's tax base

9Business combinations 19 · 66 – 68

  • FV uplift on acquiree's assets (tax base unchanged) → DTL → increases goodwill
  • No DTL on goodwill itself
  • Acquirer's own previously unrecognised DTA becomes recognisable because of the combination → P&L, not goodwill 67
  • Acquiree DTA recognised later: within measurement period & from new info about facts at acquisition → reduce goodwill (excess → P&L); otherwise → P&L 68

10Presentation & offset 71 – 76

  • Offset current tax assets / liabilities only with legally enforceable right + intent to settle net
  • Offset DTA / DTL only if legally enforceable right to set off current tax and same taxation authority (same entity, or entities intending net settlement) 74
  • DT never current (Ind AS 1 para 56)
  • Disclose tax expense reconciliation (ETR or numerical) 81(c)

11Uncertain tax treatments Appendix C

  • Assume the authority will examine with full knowledge (no detection risk)
  • Probable the authority accepts? Yes → use filed position. No → reflect uncertainty using most likely amount or expected value (whichever predicts better)
  • Consider each treatment separately or together — whichever predicts resolution better
  • Reassess when facts change (new assessment order, ruling, expiry of limitation)
