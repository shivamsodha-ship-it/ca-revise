Ind AS 16 — Property, Plant & Equipment

Cost in → depreciate by component → revalue by class → derecognise. Traps live at every arrow.

1Recognition 7 · 8 · 12 – 14

  • Recognise if probable future economic benefits + cost measured reliably 7
  • Spares, stand-by & servicing equipment → PPE if used > 1 period; else inventory 8
  • Day-to-day servicing (labour, consumables, small parts) → P&L 12
  • Replacement of a part → capitalise new part, derecognise the replaced part — even if not depreciated separately (use replacement cost as a guide to old part's cost) 13 · 70
  • Major inspection / overhaul → capitalise; derecognise remaining carrying amount of the previous inspection 14

2What goes into cost 16 – 22

IN ✓

  • Purchase price + import duties + non-refundable taxes − trade discounts/rebates
  • Directly attributable: site prep, delivery, installation, testing, professional fees, employee costs arising directly
  • Initial estimate of dismantling / restoration (Ind AS 37)
  • Borrowing costs on qualifying asset (Ind AS 23)

OUT ✗

  • Opening a new facility; new product launch / advertising
  • New location / new customer class, staff training
  • Admin & general overheads
  • Costs after asset can operate as intended: initial operating losses, relocation, under-utilisation 20
  • Abnormal waste in self-construction; incidental operations → P&L 21

2022 amendment: sale proceeds of items produced while testing are NOT deducted from cost — recognise the proceeds and their cost (Ind AS 2) in P&L. Testing cost (is it functioning properly?) is still capitalised.

3Special acquisitions

  • Deferred payment beyond normal credit → cost = cash price equivalent; difference = interest (unless Ind AS 23) 23
  • Exchange of assets → fair value, unless it lacks commercial substance or FV not reliably measurable → carrying amount of asset given up 24
  • Government grant → may deduct from cost (Ind AS 20) 28
  • Bearer plants → Ind AS 16; produce on them → Ind AS 41

4Cost model vs Revaluation model 29 – 42

  • Choice is by entire class (land, buildings, machinery…) — revalue the whole class simultaneously (rolling basis OK if completed in short period) 36 · 38
  • Carrying amount = FV at revaluation date − later acc. depreciation − later impairment 31
  • Frequency: so that carrying amount doesn't differ materially from FV

Accumulated depreciation on revaluation 35

(a) Restate gross carrying amount consistently (e.g., proportionately); acc. dep = gross − revalued amount

(b) Eliminate acc. dep against gross carrying amount; net = revalued amount

5Surplus / deficit — the sequence 39 · 40

Increase → OCI (revaluation surplus)
except: reverses an earlier P&L decrease → P&L to that extent

Decrease → P&L
except: to the extent of surplus for the SAME asset → OCI first

6Moving the surplus 41 · 42

  • Transfer to retained earnings directly (within equity) — never through P&L
  • When: on derecognition, or yearly as used = depreciation on revalued amount − depreciation on original cost
  • Deferred tax on surplus → OCI (Ind AS 12)
Surplus goes up through OCI and out through retained earnings — P&L never sees it.

7Depreciation 43 – 62A

  • Component approach — each significant part separately 43; mandatory under Sch II too
  • Land & buildings separate; land not depreciated (except quarries / landfill); restoration cost on land → over benefit period 58 · 59
  • Depreciable amount = cost − residual value; RV & useful life reviewed at least each FY-end → change = estimate 51
  • RV ≥ carrying amount → depreciation zero until RV falls 54
  • Method reviewed each FY-end → change = estimate, prospective 61
  • Revenue-based depreciation is NOT appropriate 62A
Depreciation …Rule 55

8Impairment & compensation 63 – 66

  • Impairment → Ind AS 36
  • Insurance / third-party compensation for impaired, lost or given-up PPE → P&L when receivable — separate event, never netted against the loss 65 · 66

Fire before RD: write off the asset in this year; recognise insurance only when receivable (Ind AS 37 — virtually certain). Fire after RD → Ind AS 10 non-adjusting (see Ind AS 10 notes).

9Derecognition 67 – 72

  • On disposal or when no future benefits expected
  • Gain / loss → P&L; gains are NOT revenue 68
  • Exception: PPE routinely held for rental then sold → transfer to inventory at carrying amount when rental ends; sale proceeds = revenue (Ind AS 115) 68A
  • Date of disposal & consideration → Ind AS 115 rules 69 · 72
