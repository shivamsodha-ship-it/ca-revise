Ind AS 19 — Employee Benefits

Four buckets. The bucket decides discounting and whether remeasurement goes to P&L or OCI.

Leave encashment (other long-term): actuarial valuation like DB, but actuarial gains / losses go to P&L, NOT OCI 154 – 156. Examiners love this.

2Short-term benefits 9 – 25

  • Accumulating absences (carried forward) → recognise when service increases entitlement — both vesting and non-vesting (non-vesting: adjust for expected forfeiture)
  • Non-accumulating absences (e.g., sick leave lapses) → recognise when absence occurs
  • Bonus / profit-sharing → when present legal or constructive obligation from past events + reliable estimate 19
  • Temporary change in settlement timing → no reclassification; reassess if characteristics change 10

3DC vs DB — who bears the risk?

Defined contribution

Fixed contribution; employee bears actuarial & investment risk. Expense = contribution payable for service. EPFO (RPFC) PF, ESI, NPS.

Defined benefit

Entity promises a benefit; entity bears risk. Gratuity (Payment of Gratuity Act), pensions.

PF in a company-managed exempt trust with guaranteed interest rate → DB (shortfall risk sits with employer).

4Net defined benefit liability (asset) 57 – 64

Net DBL = PV of DBO − FV of plan assets  ·  surplus capped at asset ceiling (PV of refunds / reductions in future contributions)

  • Method: Projected Unit Credit; attribute benefit per plan formula — straight-line if back-loaded
  • Discount rate: market yields on government bonds at RD 83 (Indian change; IAS 19 uses high-quality corporate bonds — foreign subs / branches use corporate bonds where deep market)

5Three components — where each goes 120

Net interestDiscount rate × net DBL (at start, adjusted for contributions / payments)P&L

P&L sees "S + I" (service + interest). OCI sees "R" (remeasurement). OCI may move to retained earnings, never to P&L.

Ind AS 19 · Plan events & terminationTRAPS

7Past service cost, curtailment, settlement 99 – 112

  • Past service cost (plan amendment / curtailment) → immediately in P&L — no spreading over vesting period (unlike old AS 15)
  • Recognise at the earlier of: amendment / curtailment occurs, or related restructuring costs (Ind AS 37) / termination benefits recognised 103
  • Settlement gain / loss = PV of DBO settled − settlement price → P&L

2019 amendment 122A

On amendment / curtailment / settlement during the year → remeasure net DBL with updated assumptions, and use those updated assumptions for current service cost & net interest for the rest of the period. Asset ceiling effect handled separately (through OCI).

Law change increasing benefits (e.g., new Labour Codes widening "wages" for gratuity) = plan amendment → past service cost → P&L immediately, not OCI. Verify applicability date for your attempt.

8Termination benefits 159 – 171

  • Arise from entity's decision to terminate or employee accepting an offer (VRS) — not from future service
  • Recognise at the earlier of: (a) entity can no longer withdraw the offer; (b) entity recognises restructuring costs (Ind AS 37) involving termination benefits 165
  • Settled within 12 m → short-term rules; otherwise → long-term (discounted)

Benefits that are conditional on future service (stay-till-closure bonus) are NOT termination benefits — attribute over the service period.

9Presentation

  • Ind AS 19 doesn't split current / non-current → apply Ind AS 1: actuary's estimate of amount payable within 12 m → current
  • Offset plan assets of one plan against another's obligation only with legal right + intent 131
  • Disclose sensitivity analysis for significant actuarial assumptions
  • Share-based payments → Ind AS 102, not Ind AS 19

  • Ind AS 2 / 16 — employee cost directly attributable may be capitalised into inventory / PPE
