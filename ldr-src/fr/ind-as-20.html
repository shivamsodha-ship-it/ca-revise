Ind AS 20 — Government Grants

Recognise when reasonably assured; match to the costs it compensates; never straight to equity.

1Recognition 7 – 9

  • Recognise only when reasonable assurance that (a) entity will comply with conditions, and (b) grant will be received 7
  • Receipt alone is not evidence that conditions have been / will be met 8
  • Recognise in P&L on a systematic basis over periods in which related costs are expensed — never directly to equity / capital reserve (income approach) 12

Old AS 12 let promoter's contribution-type grants go to capital reserve. Ind AS 20: all grants go through P&L (as income or reduced expense / depreciation). Government acting as owner → equity contribution, not a grant.

2Matching — which period? 16 – 22

3Below-market interest loan 10A

Loan recognised at FV under Ind AS 109 (PV at market rate).
Grant = proceeds received − initial Ind AS 109 carrying amount → deferred income, released per the costs it compensates.
Interest at market rate → finance cost via EIR.

5Repayment of a grant 32

Treated as a change in accounting estimate (Ind AS 8) — not a prior-period error.

Grant typeRepayment treatment
Income-relatedFirst against any unamortised deferred credit; excess → P&L immediately
Asset-relatedIncrease asset carrying amount or reduce deferred income; cumulative extra depreciation that would have been charged without the grant → P&L immediately

Example

Machine ₹100 L, life 5 yrs, grant ₹20 L (deducted from cost). After 2 yrs grant repaid.
Carrying amount now 80 × 3/5 = ₹48 L → add back ₹20 L → ₹68 L.
Depreciation without grant for 2 yrs = 40; actually charged = 32 → extra ₹8 L to P&L now.
New carrying amount ₹60 L (= 100 × 3/5) depreciated over remaining 3 yrs.

Repayment may also signal impairment of the asset — test under Ind AS 36 33.

6Scope — what is NOT Ind AS 20

  • Government assistance that can't be valued (free technical advice, guarantees) → disclose only 34 – 38
  • Income-tax benefits — tax holidays, investment tax credits, accelerated allowances → Ind AS 12
  • Government as owner (equity infusion) → equity
  • Grants on biological assets at FV less costs to sell → Ind AS 41 (unconditional → P&L when receivable; conditional → when conditions met)
  • Transactions with government that are normal trading (sales) → Ind AS 115

7Cross-standard links

  • Ind AS 16 · 28 — grant may reduce PPE cost
  • Ind AS 109 — FV of government loans; Ind AS 101 exception: below-market loans at transition use previous-GAAP carrying amount
  • Ind AS 8 — repayment = change in estimate
  • Ind AS 37 — conditions breached → repayment obligation may be a provision / contingent liability
  • Ind AS 12 — taxability of grant / deferred income creates TDs
  • Ind AS 7 — separate presentation of grant receipts
