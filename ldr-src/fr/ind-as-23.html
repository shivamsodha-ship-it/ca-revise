Ind AS 23 — Borrowing Costs

Capitalise borrowing costs directly attributable to a qualifying asset — mandatory, not a choice. Everything else → P&L.

1Definitions 5 – 7

  • Qualifying asset: necessarily takes a substantial period to get ready for intended use / sale (judgement; no fixed months in the standard) — plants, power stations, investment property, intangibles, inventories needing long production
  • Not qualifying: assets ready when acquired; financial assets; inventories produced over a short period
  • Borrowing costs include: interest via EIR (Ind AS 109), interest on lease liabilities (Ind AS 116), exchange differences on foreign-currency borrowings to the extent regarded as an adjustment to interest cost 6(e)
  • Cost of equity (incl. preference shares classified as equity) is not a borrowing cost

Exchange difference cap 6A

Adjustment to interest = (interest on equivalent local-currency borrowing) − (actual foreign-currency interest). Exchange loss capitalised up to that difference; rest → P&L. Gain / loss computed cumulatively — a later gain reverses earlier capitalised loss only up to that amount.

2Optional scope exclusions 4

  • Qualifying assets measured at fair value (e.g., biological assets)
  • Inventories manufactured in large quantities on a repetitive basis

3How much to capitalise 10 – 15

BorrowingAmount capitalised
SpecificActual borrowing costs less investment income on temporary investment of unspent funds
GeneralCapitalisation rate × expenditure on the asset (weighted for time). Rate = weighted average cost of general borrowings, excluding specific ones until the asset is substantially complete (2019 amendment)
  • Amount capitalised ≤ borrowing costs actually incurred
  • Expenditure = cash paid, assets transferred, interest-bearing liabilities assumed — reduced by progress payments & grants 18

4Commence · Suspend · Cease 17 – 25

StageRule
CommenceWhen all three: expenditure incurred + borrowing costs incurred + activities to prepare the asset undertaken (incl. technical / admin work before construction, e.g., permits)
SuspendDuring extended periods when active development is suspended. Not for temporary delays that are a necessary part of the process (e.g., high water levels typical of the region) or when substantial technical / admin work continues
CeaseWhen substantially all activities to prepare the asset are complete (minor modifications / decoration left). Parts completed & usable while rest continues → cease for that part

Holding land without development activity → no capitalisation (land itself isn't qualifying once development stops). Strike / dispute halting work for months → suspend. Monsoon break normal for the area → don't suspend.
