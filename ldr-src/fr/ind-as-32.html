Ind AS 32 — Equity vs Liability

One question: does the issuer have an unavoidable contractual obligation to give cash / another financial asset? Legal form doesn't decide — substance does.

1Financial liability — the definition 11

  • Contractual obligation to deliver cash / financial asset, or to exchange financial instruments under potentially unfavourable conditions
  • Contract settled in own equity:
    • non-derivative → liability if variable number of shares
    • derivative → liability unless fixed number of shares for fixed cash ("fixed-for-fixed")
Equity = the issuer can always say no to paying. Liability = it cannot.

3Special classification rules

  • Contingent settlement (pay if IPO fails / profits fall) → liability, unless the contingency is not genuine or arises only on liquidation 25
  • Settlement options (issuer / holder chooses cash or shares) → liability / derivative liability unless all alternatives give equity 26
  • Puttable instruments and obligations arising only on liquidation → equity if strict conditions (most subordinate class, pro-rata share…) 16A – 16D
  • Rights issue for fixed foreign-currency amount, pro-rata to all existing owners → equity 11
  • Indian carve-out: conversion option in a foreign-currency convertible bond (fixed number of shares for fixed amount in any currency) → equity (IAS 32 → derivative liability) 11

4Split at initial recognition 28 – 32

Step 1 Liability = PV of interest + principal, discounted at market rate for similar debt WITHOUT conversion

Step 2 Equity = Proceeds − Liability (residual)

Step 3 Transaction costs → allocated in proportion to the split 38

No gain / loss on initial split. Split is not revised for changes in likelihood of conversion 30

Example (rounded): 10,000 debentures × ₹100, 6% coupon, 3 years, market rate 9%
PV of coupons ₹6,00,000 × 2.5313₹15,18,777
PV of principal ₹1,00,00,000 × 0.7722₹77,21,835
Liability₹92,40,612
Equity component (1,00,00,000 − 92,40,612)₹7,59,388

Subsequently: liability at amortised cost (interest at 9% → P&L); equity component stays — may move within equity, never remeasured.

6Equity mechanics 33 – 41

  • Treasury shares (own shares reacquired, e.g., via ESOP trust consolidated) → deducted from equity; no gain / loss on purchase, sale, cancellation 33
  • Interest, dividends, gains / losses on a liability → P&L; distributions to equity holders → equity directly 35
  • Transaction costs of equity (registration, legal, underwriting) → deducted from equity; abandoned issue costs → P&L 37
  • Income tax on equity distributions / costs → per Ind AS 12 35A

7Offsetting 42

Offset financial asset & liability only if both: (a) currently legally enforceable right to set off (not contingent on a future event), and (b) intention to settle net or simultaneously.

8In consolidation

  • Written put over NCI shares (parent must buy NCI on demand) → financial liability in CFS at PV of redemption amount
  • Instrument may be equity in sub's SFS but liability in CFS if parent guarantees payment
