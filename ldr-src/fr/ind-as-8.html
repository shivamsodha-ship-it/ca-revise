2When can a policy change? 14 · 16

Only if:

  • required by an Ind AS, or
  • gives reliable & more relevant info

NOT a policy change:

  • transactions different in substance
  • transactions new or previously immaterial

5Estimates — 2023 amendment 32 – 38

  • Estimate = monetary amount subject to measurement uncertainty.
  • Change in input or measurement technique = change in estimate, unless it corrects an error 34A
  • Recognise in P&L of period of change (and future periods if both affected) 36

Hindsight ≠ error 48. Provision ₹2 cr at 31 Mar 2024 on best evidence; case settles ₹5 cr in Nov 2024 → extra ₹3 cr in FY 24-25 P&L. No restatement.

6Errors — correction mechanics 41 – 49

  • Correct in first FS approved after discovery 42:
    • restate comparatives for the period(s) the error occurred, or
    • if before earliest period presented → restate opening balances of earliest period
  • Impracticable → restate from earliest practicable period 43 – 45
  • Error in current year found before approval → just correct current figures (not a prior-period error)

Intentional immaterial errors to achieve a particular presentation → FS do not comply 41. Materiality doesn't save intent. (Audit: SA 450 — qualitatively material; SA 240.)

Disclosure of error 49

  • nature of the error
  • correction per line item and basic & diluted EPS, each prior period presented
  • correction at start of earliest period
  • if impracticable — why; no need to repeat in later years

10Exam-ready sentences

Error: "As per para 42 of Ind AS 8, a material prior period error shall be corrected retrospectively in the first set of financial statements approved for issue after its discovery, by restating the comparative amounts for the prior period presented in which the error occurred, or, if it occurred before the earliest period presented, the opening balances of assets, liabilities and equity for the earliest prior period presented."

Estimate: "The change in depreciation method is a change in accounting estimate (Ind AS 16 para 61) and shall be applied prospectively by including its effect in profit or loss of the period of change and future periods (Ind AS 8 para 36)."

AAA: "Since the corresponding figures have not been restated for a material misstatement in the prior period financial statements on which an unmodified opinion was issued, the auditor shall express a qualified or adverse opinion, modified with respect to the corresponding figures (SA 710 para 12)."
