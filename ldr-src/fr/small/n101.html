101Ind AS 101 First-time Adoption SM Ch 14 · 5-mark Q5(b) + MCQ in every paper

PointRule (SM)
Key datesFirst Ind AS FS = first annual FS with an explicit unreserved statement of compliance (Illus 3). Date of transition = beginning of the earliest comparative period (Illus 1: adopt 1 Apr 20X1 with one comparative → transition 1 Apr 20X0). Opening Ind AS balance sheet at that date. Previous GAAP = Indian GAAP (not IFRS — Illus 2).
Opening balance sheet(a) recognise all assets/liabilities Ind AS requires; (b) derecognise items Ind AS doesn't permit (e.g. proposed dividend, DDT — Jan 2026); (c) reclassify (redeemable preference shares → liability; deferred tax on temporary differences); (d) measure per Ind AS. Adjustments → retained earnings (or other equity). Same accounting policies for all periods, latest version of Ind AS (Example 2).
Mandatory exceptions (no choice)Estimates — consistent with previous GAAP estimates unless error; later information is not used (May 2026 MCQ: provision 10 lakh stays though settled for 15 lakh). Derecognition of financial assets/liabilities prospectively. Hedge accounting. NCI (Illus 4: allocate losses even if NCI goes negative — prospectively). Classification and measurement of financial assets on facts at transition. Impairment of financial assets. Embedded derivatives. Government loans below market — keep previous GAAP carrying amount (Example 4).
Optional exemptions (main)Business combinations — don't restate past combinations (goodwill at previous GAAP carrying amount, tested for impairment; if restating one, restate all later ones — TYK 1). Deemed cost of PPE/IP/intangibles: FV at transition, or previous GAAP revaluation broadly comparable to FV (Illus 11), or previous GAAP carrying value for all PPE (Illus 9; components can be allocated — Illus 10). Long-term FCY monetary items — continue previous GAAP policy (para 46/46A) for items before first period (Illus 12, 13; TYK 3, 4). Share-based payments vested before transition — not required to apply Ind AS 102 (Illus 7). Investments in subsidiaries in separate FS — cost per Ind AS 27, or deemed cost (FV or previous carrying amount). Compound financial instruments — no split if liability component no longer outstanding (May 2026 Q5(b) cites para D18). Decommissioning liabilities included in PPE. Leases — assess at transition.
Subsidiary adopting later / earlierSubsidiary adopting after parent → same carrying amounts as in parent's CFS (or its own transition) (Illus 6, 8).
DisclosuresReconciliations of equity (transition date and end of latest previous-GAAP period) and total comprehensive income; explain material cash-flow adjustments; impairments recognised.

Illus 15 / May 2026 Q5(b) (compound at transition): 6% convertible debentures, 10% premium on redemption, market 10% → split liability and equity at the date of issue; equity part to other equity (D18 exemption only if liability no longer outstanding). Illus 16 / TYK 5 / TYK 6 (equity reconciliation): start with previous GAAP equity; ± fair value of investments (FVTPL/FVOCI), − proposed dividend reversed (adds back), ± deferred tax, − redeemable preference shares to liability, ± expected credit loss, ± deemed cost uplift (Jan 2026: land at FV). TYK 6: equity on 1 Apr 20X1 after transition 182.03. Sep 2025 Q5(b): land and capitalised exchange differences under deemed cost; goodwill of an old acquisition kept (exemption).
