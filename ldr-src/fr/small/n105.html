105Ind AS 105 Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations SM Ch 5 Unit 7 · Jan 2026 6 marks, May 2026 2 MCQs

PointRule (SM)
Classify HFSCarrying amount recovered mainly through sale: (1) available for immediate sale in present condition (usual terms) and (2) sale highly probable — management committed, active programme to find buyer, actively marketed at a reasonable price, expected within 12 months, unlikely to be withdrawn.
12-month extensionDelay beyond control and still committed (e.g. buyer's or regulator's new conditions; unexpected market deterioration with price reduced). TYK 2: factory not actively marketed at a reasonable price → not HFS unless price cut.
Not HFSAbandoned assets (closed coal mines → may be a discontinued operation when abandoned — Example 12); temporarily idle plant (Example 13; Jan 2026 Q4(a)); refurbishment needed before sale beyond usual (Example 11 — HFS only after refurbishment if buyer requires it). Criteria met after year-end → not HFS at year-end (disclose).
Acquired for resaleHFS at acquisition if 12-month criterion met and others likely within 3 months. Subsidiary acquired for resale → single line at FV less costs (PQ 2: loss 20).
MeasureJust before: measure under the relevant Ind AS (depreciate up to date — Illus 1: carry at 5,60,000). Then lower of carrying amount and FV less costs to sell. Stop depreciation. Costs to sell beyond 1 year → PV; unwinding = finance cost (PQ 4).
ImpairmentWrite-down → P&L. Disposal group: allocate loss to goodwill first, then non-current assets in scope, pro rata (not to inventory, financial assets, DTA, etc.). Later gains → reverse up to cumulative losses recognised (Example 16; goodwill never reversed).
Ceases to be HFSMeasure at lower of (carrying amount as if never classified — with depreciation) and recoverable amount; adjustment → continuing operations P&L (Illus 2: 4,33,846, charge 26,154).
PresentationHFS assets and liabilities separately on the balance sheet (no offset); OCI items separately; no restatement of comparatives on the balance sheet.
Discontinued operationComponent disposed or HFS that is a separate major line of business or geographical area, part of a single plan, or a subsidiary bought only to resell. One outlet of 200 → not (Illus 4). Single amount in P&L (post-tax profit + gain/loss on remeasurement); comparatives re-presented; separate EPS.

TYK 4 (disposal group in CFS): impairment allocated to goodwill (200) first then intangibles/PPE pro rata; inventory, financial assets measured under own standards. May 2026 MCQs: goodwill after remeasurement → nil; PPE of disposal group after impairment → 30 m.
