108Ind AS 108 Operating Segments SM Ch 8 Unit 3

PointRule (SM)
ScopeSeparate/CFS of entities with debt or equity traded (or being issued) in a public market. CFS presented with separate FS → segments only in CFS.
Operating segmentComponent that (a) earns revenue and incurs expenses (incl. with other components), (b) results regularly reviewed by the CODM for resource allocation and performance, (c) discrete financial information available. Start-ups can be segments. Head office, post-employment plans → not segments.
CODMA function (CEO, COO, board, committee) that allocates resources and assesses performance — management approach.
AggregationAllowed if similar economic characteristics (margins) and similar in all of: products/services, production process, customers, distribution, regulatory environment (Illus 5: France, Italy, Germany aggregated as EU; UK and India separate; Illus 6: paper varieties → one segment).
Quantitative thresholds (10%)Reportable if any: revenue (external + internal) ≥ 10% of combined revenue of all segments; |profit or loss| ≥ 10% of the greater of (combined profit of profit-making segments, combined loss of loss-making segments); assets ≥ 10% of combined assets.
75% testExternal revenue of reportable segments ≥ 75% of entity's external revenue; else add more segments even below 10% (Illus 9: each segment 5% → at least 15 segments). Practical limit around 10.
OthersNon-reportable segments combined in "all other segments". A segment reportable last year stays reportable if management judges it significant.
Measure and discloseAmounts as reported to the CODM (even if policies differ — TYK 3: FIFO internally, weighted average externally → disclose CODM basis + reconciliation). Disclose profit/loss, assets, liabilities (if reviewed), revenue, interest, depreciation, tax, material items; reconciliations to entity totals; entity-wide: products, geography, major customers ≥ 10% of revenue.

PQ 1 (profit test): profits total 8,280 (A 780, B 1,500, E 6,000), losses total 6,800 → use 8,280: A 9% → not reportable; B 18%, C 28%, D 54%, E 72% → B, C, D, E. TYK 1: revenue test then 75%: A, B, D give 83% of external revenue → C shown as "other". Illus 8: a segment failing revenue and profit tests can still pass the asset test.
