111Ind AS 111 Joint Arrangements SM Ch 13 Unit 5

PointRule (SM)
Joint arrangementTwo or more parties bound by a contractual arrangement that gives two or more of them joint control. Contract may be in articles, minutes, agreements — need not be in writing (Illus 11: informal agreement evidenced in meetings).
Joint controlContractually agreed sharing of control; decisions about relevant activities need unanimous consent of the parties sharing control. Steps: does the contract give all parties, or a group, control collectively? → do decisions need unanimous consent of that group?
Examples (SM)50:50 with unanimous decisions → joint (Illus 1, 2 implicit). A 50%, B 30%, C 20% with 75% majority → A and B jointly control (Illus 3). 50/25/25 with 75% → more than one combination → no joint control unless contract specifies (Illus 4). 35/35/30 with simple majority → not a JA (Illus 5). Chairman's casting vote → one party controls (Illus 7). Board appointment rights decide, not voting % (Illus 6, 8). Protective rights don't count (Illus 12). Dispute resolution by one party doesn't stop joint control (Illus 13). Different activities, key ones joint → JA (Illus 10).
TypeJoint operation = parties have rights to the assets and obligations for the liabilities. Joint venture = rights to the net assets.
Classification steps(1) Not in a separate vehicle → JO (Illus 14, 15). (2) Separate vehicle: does the legal form give rights to assets/obligations (LLP/partnership with no separation → JO — Illus 17; company → JV — Illus 16)? (3) Contract terms override? (Illus 18). (4) Other facts: output sold mainly to the parties and they fund the liabilities → JO (Illus 19 reassessment).
JO accountingRecognise own share of assets, liabilities, revenue, expenses (line by line, in own and separate FS) (Illus 21: machinery 2,50,000, cash 25,000, loans). Sale to JO → gain/loss only to the extent of other parties' interests; full loss if it shows impairment (Illus 23: bank 32 + loss 8 = asset 40). Purchase from JO → don't recognise own share of the JO's gain until resold (Illus 24).
JV accountingEquity method (Ind AS 28). Party without joint control: JO with rights → recognise share; else Ind AS 109 / 28.

Ch 13 PQ 4, 9, 13, 15 and TYK 1 (convertible preference shares giving Z a veto → Y becomes a JV of X and Z → X loses control; retained interest at FV, then equity method) are included below.
