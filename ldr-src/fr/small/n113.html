113Ind AS 113 Fair Value Measurement SM Ch 4 Unit 3 · Jan 2026 4-mark, May 2026 MCQ + 4-mark

PointRule (SM)
DefinitionPrice received to sell an asset / paid to transfer a liability in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date — an exit price, market-based not entity-specific.
MarketPrincipal market (greatest volume and activity for that asset) — use it even if another market is better. No principal market → most advantageous market (best net of transaction and transport costs). Transaction costs are not deducted from FV (used only to pick the market); transport costs are deducted.
CharacteristicsInclude condition, location and restrictions that go with the asset (land restricted for 10 years — Example 9); ignore restrictions specific to the entity (Example 2).
Non-financial assetsHighest and best use — physically possible, legally permissible, financially feasible — even if the entity uses it differently (May 2026 MCQ: vacant land → HBU; TYK 2: factory land vs residential use).
Liabilities / own equityAssume transferred, not settled; include non-performance risk (own credit); no separate input for transfer restriction.
Day-1Transaction price usually = FV; differences (related party, forced sale, different unit of account, different market) → per relevant Ind AS.
TechniquesMarket (comparables, multiples), cost (replacement cost), income (DCF, option models). Use consistently; maximise observable inputs. Expected cash flows = probability-weighted (Example 16: 700×20% + 800×40% + 900×40% = 820).
HierarchyLevel 1 quoted price, identical asset, active market (no adjustment). Level 2 other observable (similar assets, inactive markets, yield curves, interest rates). Level 3 unobservable (own data, DCF with entity forecasts). Category = lowest level input significant to the whole.
Premiums/discountsControl premium / NCI discount / liquidity discount only if a market participant would apply them and consistent with unit of account (not blockage factors).
