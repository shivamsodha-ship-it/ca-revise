PointRule (SM)
Is it a lease?Contract conveys the right to control the use of an identified asset for a period for consideration: (1) identified asset — explicit or implicit (Illus 2, 3); physically distinct portion (5 fibres — Illus 5) yes; capacity portion of a pipeline no (Illus 6); not if supplier has a substantive substitution right (practical ability + economic benefit — Illus 4; TYK 1: wagons substitutable → no lease). (2) Customer gets substantially all economic benefits (tax credits ignored — Illus 8). (3) Customer directs how and for what purpose (or decisions predetermined and customer designed the asset — TYK 2). Supplier deciding → supply contract (Illus 10).
Exemptions (lessee)Short-term ≤ 12 months (no purchase option; by class) and low-value assets (per lease) → expense straight line (Illus 1: count extension options reasonably certain to be exercised when testing the 12 months).
ComponentsSeparate lease and non-lease components; allocate on relative stand-alone prices (Illus 15: 90,000 → 76,500 lease / 13,500 maintenance); lessee may elect not to separate (by class). Admin tasks/insurance passed through are not components (Illus 14).
Lease termNon-cancellable period + extension options reasonably certain to be exercised + termination options reasonably certain not to be exercised (TYK 5: significant leasehold improvements → 10 years). Reassess on significant event within lessee's control.
Lease paymentsFixed (incl. in-substance fixed — Illus 20–22) less incentives receivable; variable payments based on an index/rate (at current index — Illus 23); purchase option price if reasonably certain; termination penalties; residual value guarantee — amount expected to be payable (Illus 26: 8,000). Usage/sales-based variable payments → P&L when incurred.
Lessee initialLease liability = PV of unpaid payments at rate implicit (else incremental borrowing rate). ROU asset = liability + payments made at/before commencement − incentives + initial direct costs + dismantling/restoration estimate (Illus 28: 8,11,000; Illus 29).
Lessee laterLiability: + interest (EIR) − payments. ROU: depreciate over shorter of lease term and useful life (useful life if ownership transfers / purchase option certain). Remeasure liability for term/option changes (revised rate) and index changes (unchanged rate), adjust ROU (Illus 32; PQ 3: +22,854).
ModificationsSeparate lease if scope increases with a price at stand-alone price (Illus 33). Otherwise remeasure liability at revised rate: decrease in scope → reduce ROU proportionately, gain/loss to P&L (Illus 35); extension or price change → adjust ROU (Illus 34: +2,50,745; Illus 36).
Lessor classificationFinance lease if substantially all risks and rewards transfer (ownership transfer, bargain purchase option, major part of economic life, PV ≥ substantially all FV, specialised asset). Else operating lease.
Lessor accountingFinance lease: net investment (PV of payments + unguaranteed residual) → finance income. Manufacturer/dealer lessor: revenue = lower of FV and PV of payments at a market rate; cost of sales = cost − PV of unguaranteed residual; initial costs expensed (Illus 38; May 2026 Q3(a): revenue 57,26,800). Operating lease: income straight line, depreciate asset, incentives spread.
SubleaseClassify by reference to the ROU asset of the head lease, not the underlying asset (Illus 39). Finance sublease → derecognise ROU, recognise net investment; keep head lease liability (Illus 40).
Sale and leasebackTransfer is a sale (Ind AS 115)? Yes → derecognise asset; ROU = proportion of previous carrying amount retained; gain only on rights transferred; off-market terms adjusted (Illus 42). No → financial liability.
