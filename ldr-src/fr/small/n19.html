19Ind AS 19 Employee Benefits SM Ch 6 Unit 1 · 19 marks in 3 papers: Sep 2025 8 (DBO), Jan 2026 5 (short-term), May 2026 6 (DBO 3 years)

TypeRule (SM)
Short-term (settled within 12 months)Undiscounted expense as service is rendered. Compensated absences: accumulating → liability for the expected extra payment from unused entitlement at year-end; vesting (paid on exit) → full unused balance; non-vesting → only the part expected to be used (attrition considered); non-accumulating (maternity, sick that lapses) → only when absence occurs. Profit-sharing/bonus → when present legal/constructive obligation and reliable estimate (Illus 7: 4.5% × 200 cr = 9 cr).
Defined contributionEntity's obligation limited to contributions; risk with employee → expense = contribution payable for the period (Illus 11: unpaid 3.20 cr → liability). Multi-employer / state plans: classify by terms; DB plan without enough information → treat as DC + disclose (Example 1).
Defined benefit — steps(1) estimate benefit (actuarial, projected unit credit method); (2) discount at market yields on government bonds (Ind AS carve-out; IFRS uses high-quality corporate bonds — ICAI's Sep 2025 paper corrected exactly this); (3) FV of plan assets; (4) net DB liability/asset = DBO − plan assets, limited by asset ceiling (Illus 14: lower of surplus and ceiling 175); (5) P&L: service cost + net interest; (6) OCI: remeasurements.
AttributionTo periods of service under the plan formula; if later years give materially higher benefit → straight line. Vesting after 10 years → attribute evenly over 10 years (Illus 16: 150 per year) reflecting probability; service before age 25 excluded (Illus 17); no benefit after the date further service adds nothing material (Illus 19, 22). Post-employment medical plans with a higher % after longer service → attribute up to the point after which further service adds no material benefit, reflecting the probability of leaving (Illus 20, 21).
P&L itemsCurrent service cost; past service cost (plan amendment or curtailment) and gain/loss on settlement → immediately when they occur; net interest = net liability × discount rate (start of year, adjusted for contributions/benefits during year).
OCI (never recycled)Remeasurements: actuarial gains/losses on DBO; return on plan assets excluding interest income; change in asset ceiling effect.
Other long-term (long-service leave, long-term compensated absences)Same measurement as DB but everything in P&L (no OCI).
Termination benefitsRecognise at earlier of: can no longer withdraw the offer, and restructuring costs recognised (Ind AS 37). Benefits that need further service → not termination benefits (Example 4).
Change in methodGratuity on payment basis is wrong (Illus 12). First actuarial valuation showing past error → Ind AS 8 (Illus 23).
DBO reconciliation (Sep 2025, May 2026, PQ 2/3/4/6): Opening DBO + interest cost + current service cost + past service cost − benefits paid ± settlement → expected closing; actual closing − expected = actuarial loss/(gain) to OCI. Plan assets: opening FV + interest income + contributions − benefits paid → expected; actual − expected = return excluding interest (OCI). P&L = service costs + net interest. Balance sheet = closing DBO − closing plan assets.
