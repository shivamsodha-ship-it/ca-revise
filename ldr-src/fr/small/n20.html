20Ind AS 20 Government Grants SM Ch 10 Unit 2 · 8 marks in Jan 2026 and May 2026

PointRule (SM)
Recognise whenReasonable assurance that (a) entity will comply with conditions and (b) grant will be received. Receipt alone is not proof of compliance.
ApproachIncome approach only — never directly to equity (TYK 4: promoter-government contributions recognised as income/deferred income unless they are equity contributions by owners).
TimingIn P&L on a systematic basis over the periods the related costs are expensed. Depreciable asset → over its useful life in proportion to depreciation. Non-depreciable asset with conditions (land + build factory) → over the life of the building (Example 5).
Compensation for past costs / immediate supportNo future costs → P&L when receivable (Illus 1: past H1N1 research → immediately; PQ 3: compensation for past losses → year receivable; May 2026: start-up grant → immediately).
Future costsIllus 2: grant for R&D over 2 years → deferred income, P&L over 2 years. Training grant (PQ 1): match with training costs (or deduct from them).
Asset grants — presentationEither deferred income (released over life) or deduct from carrying amount (lower depreciation). Cash flow: show asset purchase and grant receipt separately (investing) — Illus 8.
Income grants — presentationEither as other income, or deducted from the related expense.
Non-monetary grantsLand etc. at fair value (asset + deferred income), or at nominal value (Illus 6: FV option or nominal 50,000). Jan 2026 / May 2026: free land → FV as deferred income or nominal.
Below-market government loanLoan at FV (Ind AS 109 — PV at market rate); benefit (cash − FV) = grant → recognise as per the costs it compensates (Illus 4: 50 lakh at 5% vs 12% → FV 37,38,328; grant 12,61,672; Illus 5: over 4 years or with depreciation). Interest-free loan (PQ 5): FV 4,32,000, unwind at 5%.
RepaymentChange in estimate (prospective): first against unamortised deferred income, excess → P&L. Asset grant deducted from cost → increase asset; cumulative extra depreciation → P&L now (Illus 10).
Government assistanceFree advice, guarantees, procurement policy — no value; disclose. Forgivable loan → grant when reasonable assurance of forgiveness.
