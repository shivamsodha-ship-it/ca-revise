PointRule (SM)
Functional currencyCurrency of the primary economic environment: primary — currency that mainly influences sales prices and costs (labour, materials); secondary — financing and receipts retained. Foreign operation: is it an extension of the parent (no autonomy, mostly parent transactions, cash flows affect parent, needs parent funding) → parent's currency (Illus 2, 3). Pricing in USD for convenience doesn't make USD functional (Illus 1).
InitialSpot rate on transaction date (average rate for a period allowed if rates don't fluctuate much).
Year-endMonetary items (cash, receivables, payables, loans) → closing rate; non-monetary at cost (PPE, inventory, advances paid/received) → historical rate; non-monetary at FV → rate at FV date.
Exchange differencesMonetary items → P&L. Non-monetary FV gain in OCI → FX part also OCI. Impairment/NRV of foreign-currency asset: compare cost × historical rate with recoverable amount × closing rate (Example 1).
Advance consideration (Appendix B)Advance paid/received is non-monetary → revenue/expense for that part at the advance date rate (PQ 1: USD 20 m advance at the advance-date rate; balance 30 m receivable at the delivery-date rate, then closing rate).
Net investment in foreign operationMonetary item with no planned settlement (long-term loan, perpetual debt — TYK 2): FX difference → P&L in separate FS, OCI (FCTR) in CFS (Illus 5; also via another group entity — Illus 6). Jan 2026 MCQ: loss in parent's standalone P&L.
Translation to presentation currencyAssets and liabilities at closing rate; income and expenses at transaction (or average) rates; equity at historical; difference → OCI (FCTR); goodwill and FV adjustments of a foreign operation are its assets → closing rate (PYQ May 2022: goodwill USD 0.30 m × 73.5).
DisposalLoss of control: recycle FCTR (parent's share) to P&L; NCI share derecognised with NCI (TYK 1: 1,500 − 1,000 + 100 + 180 = 780; May 2026 MCQ: 970). Partial disposal keeping control → re-attribute to NCI.
Intra-groupIntra-group monetary balances don't eliminate FX differences (group exposed — Illus 7). Unrealised profit at rate on transaction date (Illus 8: 166 lakh eliminated).
