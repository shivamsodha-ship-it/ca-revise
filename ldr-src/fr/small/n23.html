23Ind AS 23 Borrowing Costs SM Ch 5 Unit 3 · Sep 2025 6 marks, May 2026 5 marks

PointRule (SM)
Core ruleBorrowing costs directly attributable to acquisition, construction or production of a qualifying asset → capitalise; others → expense.
Borrowing costs includeInterest (EIR method), finance charges on leases, exchange differences to the extent they adjust interest (limit = interest at local-currency borrowing rate — Example 2/3, Illus 6: only part capitalised, rest expensed). Not: dividends on equity, issue costs of equity, preference shares classified as equity (Example 1).
Qualifying assetNecessarily takes a substantial period to get ready for use or sale (generally 12 months+): plants, buildings, investment property under construction, inventories with long production (cheese, whisky — Illus 1, Example 7), intangibles under development. Not: assets ready on acquisition (Illus 4: permit + land), routine short-term inventories, financial assets.
Specific borrowingsActual borrowing cost of the period − investment income on temporary investment of those funds.
General borrowingsCapitalisation rate = weighted average cost of general borrowings (excluding specific borrowings until the specific asset is substantially ready) × weighted expenditure on the asset; capitalised amount ≤ actual general borrowing costs (PQ 3: limit 0.5 cr). Specific loan left after the asset is ready → becomes general (Illus 8).
CommencementWhen all three: expenditure incurred, borrowing costs incurred, activities to prepare the asset started (Illus 10: 19 June).
SuspensionExtended periods when active development stops (not for temporary delays that are a normal part of the process, or for technical/administrative work going on) (Example 4).
CessationWhen substantially all activities to prepare the asset are complete; for parts usable separately → stop for each part completed (Example 5; PQ 4: phases; May 2026 Q6(b): floors I–III completed, IV–V not).
GroupInter-company loan funding a subsidiary's asset → in CFS capitalise based on the group's external borrowing costs (Illus 11; TYK 6: only 1,68,000).
