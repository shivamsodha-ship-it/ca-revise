27Ind AS 27 Separate Financial Statements SM Ch 13 Unit 7 · Jan 2026 Q2(a) asked standalone vs consolidated treatment

PointRule (SM)
WhatStatements of a parent / investor in which investments in subsidiaries, JVs and associates are accounted for on a basis other than consolidation/equity method. Not mandatory by Ind AS 27 itself (required by law). FS of an entity with no subsidiaries/associates are not "separate FS".
Measurement choice (per category)(a) cost, (b) Ind AS 109 (FV), or (c) equity method (Ind AS 28) — same policy for each category (Example 1: subsidiary at cost, JV at FV allowed). Held for sale → Ind AS 105 (if at cost).
DividendsRecognise in P&L when right to receive is established (or reduce carrying amount if equity method).
Investment entitySubsidiaries at FVTPL in separate FS. Ceases to be one → FV on that date = deemed cost (Example 2). Becomes one → FV; difference with previous carrying amount → P&L (Example 3).
Group reorganisation (new parent)New parent inserted by share exchange, no change in assets or interests → new parent measures investment at the carrying amount of its share of equity in the original parent's separate FS (Illus 1: 15 lakh).
Common linksInterest-free loan to subsidiary: separate FS — loan at PV + difference as investment; CFS — eliminated (Ch 11 notes §7). Loss of control in separate FS: gain = proceeds − cost of portion sold (Ch 13 Illus 31–33: e.g. 37.5 lakh).
DisclosureThat they are separate FS; list of investments with place, % holding, method used; reason if parent didn't prepare CFS under exemption.

Ch 13 PQ 6, 7 (separate FS measurement; change from non-investment to investment entity status) are included below.
