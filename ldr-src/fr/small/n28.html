28Ind AS 28 Associates and Joint Ventures SM Ch 13 Unit 6 · 10 compulsory MCQ marks: Jan 2026 (3 MCQs), May 2026 (2 MCQs)

PointRule (SM)
Significant influencePower to participate in financial and operating policy decisions (not control). Presumed at ≥ 20% voting power (direct or through subsidiaries); < 20% presumed not, unless shown. Evidence: board representation, participation in policy-making (Illus 3: 10% but in policy decisions → SI), material transactions (Illus 4: 15% but buys 90% of output → SI), interchange of managers (Illus 5), essential technical information (Illus 6: 10% → SI). Another investor with majority doesn't preclude SI, but Illus 1 (25%, others act together) → no SI. Include potential voting rights currently exercisable (Illus 7).
Equity methodInitially at cost; then + share of profit or loss and OCI − dividends received. Goodwill sits inside the carrying amount (not amortised, not tested separately); bargain → income in the year of acquisition (Jan 2026 MCQ).
Adjustments to share of profitUse FV at acquisition: extra depreciation on FV uplift (Illus 10: share 8,750; OCI 2,500; closing 1,36,250; Jan 2026 MCQ: profit after FV depreciation on building). Cumulative preference shares held by others → deduct their dividend whether declared or not (Illus 11: 50% × 3,00,000 = 1,50,000). Uniform policies; reporting date gap ≤ 3 months. Group share = parent + subsidiaries (not other associates' holdings; Example 2: 15% + 10% = 25%, JV's 10% ignored).
Unrealised profitUpstream and downstream: eliminate only the investor's share (Illus 13: 40% → 16,000). Downstream loss showing impairment → recognise fully (Illus 14). May 2026 MCQ: downstream unrealised profit reduces carrying amount before impairment test.
LossesStop when interest (incl. long-term interests like preference shares, long-term loans) is nil; further losses only if legal/constructive obligation. Later profits restart only after unrecognised losses are covered (Illus 15).
ImpairmentWhole carrying amount tested as one asset under Ind AS 36 when Ind AS 28 indicators exist (May 2026 MCQ: 40% of recoverable 1,15,00,000 vs carrying → loss 24,94,000, ICAI key B).
ExemptionsVenture capital / mutual funds / unit trusts may measure at FVTPL (Illus 9; Example 1). Parent exempt from CFS (Ind AS 110.4 conditions). Held for sale → Ind AS 105 (Illus 17).
DiscontinueCeases to be associate/JV → retained interest at FV; gain/loss = FV retained + proceeds − carrying amount → P&L; recycle OCI (Example 5) (Illus 16: gain 1,00,000). Associate ↔ JV → keep equity method, no remeasurement. Dilution (TYK 3): gain = share of new issue proceeds − proportion of carrying amount (160 − 120 = 40).
Carrying amount (May 2026 MCQ 6): cost 50,00,000 + share of year's profit 45% × 16,00,000 = 57,20,000 (revaluation before acquisition is in cost, not in share). Chain (TYK 2): O's profit 40 × 25% = 10 → 9 to M, 1 to N's NCI.
