PointRule (SM)
ScopeEntities with ordinary shares traded or being issued publicly; in CFS, EPS on parent's profit. Present basic and diluted EPS (continuing and total) on the face of P&L, equal prominence, even if negative.
Basic — numeratorProfit attributable to ordinary equity holders of the parent − preference dividends: cumulative → the year's dividend whether declared or not; non-cumulative → only declared. Premium on buy-back of preference shares above carrying amount → deduct (Example 5); discount → add. Increasing-rate preference shares: amortise discount (Illus 1: deduct 1,00,000 in year 3).
Basic — denominatorWeighted average shares outstanding (time-weighted from date consideration is receivable). Partly paid → fraction to the extent entitled to dividend (Illus 17: 1.75/2.50 → 70,000). Contingently issuable → when conditions satisfied. Shares issued in business combination → from acquisition date. Treasury shares excluded.
Bonus / split / consolidationNo new resources → adjust shares as if from the beginning of the earliest period presented; restate prior-year EPS (Illus 4: 10 paise × 4,000/5,000 = 8 paise; Illus 6: 35 p × 9/10 = 31.5 p). Two-for-one bonus → multiply by three (Example 7).
Rights issueBonus element factor = fair value per share cum-rights ÷ theoretical ex-rights price (TERP = (old shares × cum price + new × rights price) ÷ total shares). Apply factor to shares before the issue (TYK 1: TERP 0.9833 → 1,154,237 shares; TYK 4: 20X1 EPS 2.54).
Diluted — numeratorAdd back post-tax effect of dividends, interest and other changes from converting dilutive potential shares (interest × (1 − tax)).
Diluted — denominator+ potential shares from the start of the year (or issue date). Options/warrants (treasury stock method): only the "free" shares = shares issued − shares that the exercise proceeds would buy at average market price; dilutive only if exercise price < average price (TYK 3: 2,00,000 − 1,50,000 = 50,000 free). Convertibles → full number of shares, add back interest net of tax. Mandatorily convertible → included in basic from issue (Jan 2026 MCQ). Contingently issuable → if conditions would be met at year-end.
Anti-dilutionInclude only if it reduces EPS (or increases loss per share) from continuing operations; test in order of most dilutive first (earnings per incremental share). Loss from continuing operations → potential shares anti-dilutive (Illus 13).
OtherRetrospective adjustment for bonus after year-end before approval. Participating preference / two classes → two-class method (Illus 15: 7.20 and 6.40 per share). Subsidiary's convertibles in group diluted EPS (TYK 5).
