36Ind AS 36 Impairment of Assets SM Ch 5 Unit 4 · every paper: Sep 2025 5 marks, Jan 2026 MCQ, May 2026 MCQ (CGUs + corporate asset)

PointRule (SM)
When to testIndicators at each reporting date (external: market value fall, adverse changes, interest rates up, market cap below net assets; internal: obsolescence, damage, idle, worse performance). Annually regardless: goodwill, indefinite-life intangibles, intangibles not yet available for use (any time in the year, same time each year — Example 1).
Recoverable amountHigher of fair value less costs of disposal and value in use (Illus 1: 250 vs 238 → 250). Either above carrying amount → no impairment.
Value in usePV of future cash flows from continuing use and disposal, current condition (exclude future restructuring not committed and enhancements; exclude financing and tax); budgets ≤ 5 years; pre-tax discount rate reflecting time value and asset-specific risks; foreign-currency flows discounted in that currency, translated at spot.
Recognise lossCarrying amount − recoverable amount → P&L (revalued assets: first against revaluation surplus via OCI). Then revise depreciation over remaining life (Illus 12: 12 lakh / 3 = 4 lakh). Deferred tax recalculated (Illus 5).
CGUSmallest group of assets with largely independent cash inflows (Illus 14: each magazine title; 15: railway → whole mine; 16: bus routes → whole company; 17–18: integrated plants together). If an active market exists for output → separate CGU even if used internally.
GoodwillAllocate to CGUs expected to benefit (not larger than an operating segment) (Illus 7: 8 and 7). Test annually. Partial goodwill (NCI at proportionate share) → gross up goodwill for NCI before testing; loss on unrecognised NCI goodwill not recognised (Illus 11; TYK 3).
Corporate assetsAllocate to CGUs on a reasonable basis if possible; test each CGU including its share; else test the smallest group of CGUs to which it can be allocated (TYK 2; May 2026 MCQ: carrying 80 and 120, recoverable 72 and 152, building 40).
Allocate a CGU lossFirst goodwill, then other assets pro rata to carrying amounts; no asset below the highest of its FV less costs, value in use and zero (Illus 8: 4/24 building, 20/24 others; Illus 20: goodwill 200 + assets 300).
ReversalIndicators reverse → recoverable amount up → reverse up to carrying amount net of depreciation had no impairment been recognised (Illus 10: 2,56,000; depreciation 16,000). Goodwill impairment never reversed. Unwinding of discount alone is not a reversal (TYK 5).

Illus 3 / PQ 5: VIU = Σ cash flows × discount factors; compare with FV less costs; recognise loss; revised depreciation. TYK 4: machinery 100 cr, 5 years → impaired to 75.31 cr, depreciated over 4 years. PQ 1–3: expected cash flow = probability-weighted (220; PV 892.36).
