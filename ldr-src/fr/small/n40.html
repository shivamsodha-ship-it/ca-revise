40Ind AS 40 Investment Property SM Ch 5 Unit 6

PointRule (SM)
DefinitionLand/building (or part) held by owner or lessee (ROU) to earn rentals or for capital appreciation or both — not for own use (Ind AS 16) and not for sale in ordinary business (Ind AS 2).
Is IPLand held for long-term appreciation; land with undetermined future use; building leased out under operating lease; vacant building held to be leased; property being built for future use as IP.
Not IPProperty for sale in business (inventory); owner-occupied incl. employee housing; property leased to another entity under a finance lease; property being built for others.
Part own usePortions separately sellable / finance-leasable → split; if not, IP only if own-use portion is insignificant (Sun Ltd: 10 of 15 floors leased → IP for those 10).
Ancillary servicesInsignificant (security, maintenance) → IP; significant (hotel) → owner-occupied.
GroupBuilding leased to a group company: IP in its separate FS, PPE in the CFS.
Initial costPurchase price + directly attributable (legal, transfer taxes). Exclude start-up costs, initial operating losses, abnormal waste. Deferred payment → cash price; difference = interest (Illus 4: 1 cr; 20 lakh interest over 2 years).
ExchangeAt FV unless no commercial substance / FV not reliable (Example 5).
SubsequentCost model only under Ind AS 40 (depreciate as Ind AS 16; disclose FV). Replacement part: add new cost, remove old (Illus 1: 50 + 5 − 1 = 54 lakh). Day-to-day servicing → expense.
TransfersOnly on change in use (evidence: start of owner-occupation, start of development for sale, end of owner-occupation, inception of operating lease). Intention alone is not enough. Under cost model, transfers at carrying amount.
DisposalGain/loss = net proceeds − carrying amount → P&L (Example 6).
DiscloseModel, FV of IP (and how determined, valuer), rental income, direct expenses, restrictions.

Illus 3: building 30 lakh + transfer tax/legal → cost 39,80,000 (borrowing cost not capitalised — not a qualifying asset). Illus 2: office space + club membership/fees needed to buy → cost 5,06,25,000. TYK 1: 40 lakh, RV 2 lakh, 20 yrs → dep 1,90,000; carrying 38,10,000. TYK 3: 6 houses, own use for one → split cost. TYK 4: land developed into flats for sale → inventory at lower of cost (2.6 cr) and NRV. PQ 1: land with undetermined use → IP; when construction of own office starts → transfer to PPE.
