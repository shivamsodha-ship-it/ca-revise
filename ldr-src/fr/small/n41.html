41Ind AS 41 Agriculture SM Ch 10 Unit 1

PointRule (SM)
ScopeAgricultural activity = management of the biological transformation and harvest of biological assets for sale or conversion. Covers biological assets (living animals/plants) and agricultural produce at the point of harvest. After harvest → Ind AS 2.
Out of scopeBearer plants (→ Ind AS 16; their produce is in scope), land (16/40), intangibles, government grants on bearer plants (Ind AS 20), processing after harvest (wine from grapes — Illus 1).
Not agricultural activityZoo animals, ocean fishing, catching fish in ponds without managing growth (PQ 2), forest harvesting of unmanaged forests. Fish farming is in scope (TYK 5). Leasing orchards and managing the trees yourself is agricultural activity (PQ 1).
RecogniseControl from past events + probable benefits + FV or cost reliably measurable.
MeasureFair value less costs to sell at initial recognition and every reporting date. Exception: FV not reliably measurable at initial recognition → cost less depreciation/impairment until FV becomes reliable.
Gains/lossesInitial recognition and later changes in FV less costs to sell → P&L (a day-1 loss arises because costs to sell are deducted; calf born → gain — Example 3).
ProduceAt point of harvest: FV less costs to sell = cost for Ind AS 2 (Illus 5: milk 3,000 × 24 = 72,000).
GrantsUnconditional grant on biological asset at FV less costs → P&L when receivable; conditional → when conditions met (Example 4: 10 cr for 5 years' cultivation, refundable → recognise after 5 years). If asset at cost → Ind AS 20.

Illus 3: 100 sheep for 5,00,000, 2% selling cost → initial FV less costs 4,90,000 (loss 10,000); FV later rises → gain 98,000. TYK 1: cattle bought 1,00,000 + transport 1,000; FV 1,10,000 less transport and 2% fee → gain 9,800. TYK 4: goats 10 lakh, 2% cost, FV falls to 9 lakh → loss 98,000 [9,80,000 − (9,00,000 − 18,000)]. PQ 3 (coconut palms): bearer plants under Ind AS 16; nuts on the tree are produce valued at FV less costs → ₹ 231 cr.
