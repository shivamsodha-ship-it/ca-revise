CFConceptual Framework for Financial Reporting SM Ch 2 · 4-mark either/or in Sep 2025 and May 2026

PointWhat to write
Status / purposeNot an Ind AS; doesn't override any Ind AS. Helps standard-setters, helps preparers develop policies where no Ind AS applies, helps users understand. Objective of general-purpose reports: useful information to existing and potential investors, lenders and other creditors for decisions about providing resources (economic resources, claims, changes; stewardship).
Fundamental qualitative characteristicsRelevance — predictive value, confirmatory value (Example 1: revenue), materiality (entity-specific). Faithful representation — complete (Example 2), neutral (supported by prudence — caution, no asymmetry), free from error (estimates fine if described — Example 3). Apply: relevant first, then faithfully representable.
Enhancing characteristics (May 2026)Comparability (across entities and periods; consistency helps), verifiability (direct or indirect; independent observers could agree), timeliness, understandability (clear, concise; users with reasonable knowledge). Cost constraint applies.
Reporting entity / going concernConsolidated, unconsolidated or combined statements. Prepared on going concern unless intention/need to liquidate.
ElementsAsset = present economic resource controlled as a result of past events; economic resource = right with potential to produce economic benefits (Example 4: land — several rights; Example 6: receivable from bankrupt customer still a right). Liability = present obligation to transfer an economic resource as a result of past events (no practical ability to avoid; executory contract — Example 7: salary for services not yet received → no liability). Equity = residual. Income/expenses = changes in assets/liabilities other than from equity holders (Sep 2025).
Recognition / derecognitionRecognise if it meets the definition and gives relevant information + faithful representation (existence uncertainty, low probability, measurement uncertainty, cost constraint). Derecognise when control/obligation is lost; retained exposure may need continued recognition (Illus 1: receivables factored with recourse → keep asset, show liability).
Measurement basesHistorical cost and current value: fair value, value in use (assets) / fulfilment value (liabilities), current cost. Factors: relevance, faithful representation, measurement uncertainty. Initial measurement of off-market transactions (May 2026 OR): deemed cost; difference to income/expense (Example 9: interest-free loan to subsidiary). At-market: cash or exchange of assets at transaction price.
Presentation, capital maintenanceAggregation and classification (Illus 4). Financial capital maintenance (money or constant purchasing power — Example 11, 12) vs physical capital maintenance (operating capability — Example 13).

TYK 1: shareholder wants everything at fair value → measurement basis chosen by relevance and faithful representation; high measurement uncertainty can make FV less useful. Illus 2: recognition criteria and differences with Ind AS — Ind AS prevails. Illus 3: fair value not always used — mixed measurement is consistent with the Framework.
