EProfessional and Ethical Duty of a CA SM Ch 16 · MCQ + 5-mark case in every paper

PointWhat to write
5 fundamental principles (Code of Ethics)Integrity (straightforward, honest; not associated with false/misleading information) · Objectivity (no bias, conflict of interest or undue influence) · Professional competence and due care · Confidentiality · Professional behaviour (comply with laws, avoid discrediting the profession).
5 threatsSelf-interest (financial interest, bonus tied to profit, fear of losing job) · Self-review · Advocacy · Familiarity · Intimidation (pressure from directors/CEO to change numbers). May 2026 MCQ: no motive/opportunity to manipulate price-sensitive information is NOT a self-interest threat.
Conceptual framework approachIdentify threats → evaluate (acceptable level? reasonable and informed third party test) → address: eliminate the circumstance, apply safeguards, or decline/withdraw.
Professional accountant in business (CFO / FC — most exam cases)Prepare and present information faithfully per Ind AS; don't manipulate (channel stuffing, deferring expenses, under-provision, misclassification, window dressing to meet covenants or bonus targets). Pressure to breach: discuss with superior → escalate to audit committee / those charged with governance → seek legal or ICAI advice → consider resigning. Document.
Other dutiesAct within sufficient expertise; financial interests and inducements (decline gifts that influence); conflicts of interest (disclose, consent, safeguards — Jan 2026 MCQ: two partners of a dissolving firm); NOCLAR (non-compliance with laws) — respond, escalate, consider disclosure to authority.
CA Act linksSecond Schedule Part I/II: a member in employment who doesn't exercise due diligence or is grossly negligent → professional misconduct; Clause 1 Part II: contravening the Act/Regulations/Council guidelines. Council guidelines: a member who is an employee shall exercise due diligence.
How to answer a case (5 marks): (1) correct Ind AS treatment of each issue with para reference; (2) principles at risk (integrity, objectivity, competence/due care, professional behaviour); (3) threats (self-interest — bonus; intimidation — CEO pressure); (4) what the CA should do (refuse, explain correct treatment, escalate to audit committee, document, resign if needed); (5) consequence: professional misconduct under the CA Act.

SM Illus 1–9 are all this pattern: bonus on profit (Infostar — revenue / cash-flow manipulation), decommissioning provision understated (Rustom), window dressing before capital raise (Alaap), covenant breach hidden (Sunshine), discontinued operation / held for sale misused (Mitra), cash generated from operations inflated for incentive (Spinz).
