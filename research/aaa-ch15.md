last 5 years (Dec 2021 attempt onward).

## Sources

Ch 15: https://resource.cdn.icai.org/87922bos-aps2168-ch15.pdf. ICAI lists that edition as "Applicable for May 2026 Exam Onwards":
https://www.icai.org/post/sm-final-p3-may2026

**Currency.** No amendments affect this chapter. The Academic Updates in the Nov 2026, May 2026 and Jan 2026 RTPs cover
only Ch 14 (NBFC) and Ch 19.

**Where the papers came from.**
- The Nov 2026 RTP is the user's own copy (ICAI file 93965bos-aps6169-rtp-nov2026-ch3.pdf).
- Other RTPs, question papers and suggested answers are ICAI's own PDFs on resource.cdn.icai.org, found through
  icai.org and the index https://www.vsijaipur.com/ca-final-papers/.

  ("Mock Test Paper – Series I/II"), Paper 3, and a paper date matching ICAI's BoS schedule
  (e.g. May 2026 S-I on 20 Mar 2026 and S-II on 6 Apr 2026).
