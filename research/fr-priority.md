# FR: which chapters ICAI tests most (sweep for the Nov 2026 attempt)

Built 14 Oct 2026 from 35 ICAI papers for Paper 1 Financial Reporting, May 2022 to Nov 2026: 8 question papers with ICAI
suggested answers (May 2022 – May 2026; the May 2026 file is the question paper), 12 RTPs (May 2022 – Nov 2026) and 15 MTPs
(Nov 2022 – Nov 2026). ICAI PDFs come from resource.cdn.icai.org; MTPs not on the CDN are the Google Drive copies linked by
vsijaipur.com/ca-final-papers, each checked by its header (ICAI "Mock Test Paper – Series I/II", Paper 1, month and year).

**Method (app-written, approximate).** A unit counts as tested in a paper when its Ind AS number or its key terms
(for example "deferred tax", "right-of-use", "performance obligation") appear at least three times in ICAI's answers.
Score = 2 × question papers + RTP/MTP appearances + 1 if in the Nov 2026 RTP or MTPs (the skill's priority rule applied
per unit). This orders the build; it is not a marks count. Exact per-question priority is done inside each chapter's
research file. Broad terms ("associate", "inventory") inflate some units slightly.

**Could not fetch (ICAI files not publicly reachable from here):** suggested answers Nov 2023; MTPs May 2022, Nov 2023
Series II, May 2024 Series I and II, Nov 2024 Series I and II. Two "Nov 2023" files on the index were a coaching
institute's papers, not ICAI's, and were dropped. The index's "May 2024 MTP" links were May 2025 Series I duplicates.
