## 1. Marks check (replaces the old "sums to 100" check)

Each paper prints 30 MCQ marks + Q1 (compulsory, 14) + five choice questions (Q2-Q6, 14 each; 70) = **114 marks set**, plus the 4-mark OR alternative of Q6(c) = 118 printed. The candidate answers Q1 plus any four of Q2-Q6, so the marks set exceed 100; a candidate **attempts 100** (30 MCQ + Q1 + four choice questions of 14 each = 30 + 14 + 56).

| Sep 2025 | 30 | 14 | 70 (each of Q2-Q6 = 14) | 4 | 118 | 100 |
| Jan 2026 | 30 | 14 | 70 (each of Q2-Q6 = 14) | 4 | 118 | 100 |
| May 2026 | 30 | 14 | 70 (each of Q2-Q6 = 14) | 4 | 118 | 100 |

Every question and sub-part in all three papers could be read. Q2-Q6 each total 14 in every attempt (5 + 5 + 4; Q6(c) is an EITHER/OR of 4 marks each, the OR alternative is counted once in "printed extra"). Marks for Sep 2025 sub-parts are as printed (Q2(b) 2+3, Q2(c) 2+2, Q4(a) 3+2). In Jan 2026 Q4(a) and May 2026 Q2(a), Q5(b) the paper prints only the total for the sub-parts, so one row is used. Nothing was unreadable. The 15 MCQs per attempt are 2 marks each in all three papers.

**Compulsory vs choice.** Rows marked **Q1 (compulsory)** are certain to be attempted; everything else in Part II is a choice (the candidate picks 4 of 5). A topic in Q1 therefore has a 100% chance of being attempted; a topic only in Q2-Q6 has roughly a 4-in-5 chance if the question is chosen at all, and depends on whether the candidate feels it is a strong area. MCQs are all compulsory.

## 3(c). What ICAI keeps asking, top 10 chapters by marks set

**1. Ch 19 Professional Ethics and Liabilities of Auditors** (71 marks set of 354). Always the largest block (about a fifth of every paper) and nearly always scenario-advise: a short fact pattern, then "comment with reference to the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 and Schedules". The Clause-by-Clause pattern repeats: Clause 6 Part I First Schedule (tender Sep 2025, sponsorship Jan 2026) and Clause 7 with the Advertisement Guidelines 2008 (May 2026), Clause 10 (contingent fees; client monies in a separate account), Clauses 8-9 (incoming auditor), Section 2(2)(iv) (permitted services), and Council / Ethical Standards Board decisions on what assignments are allowed (asked as MCQs every time). Typical marks: 4-5 per descriptive question and 2 per MCQ; Sep 2025 and Jan 2026 also asked networking, NOCLAR and Code threats in a few marks. In May 2026 the descriptive ethics questions were Q2(b) (Clause 7 and advertisement guidelines), Q3(b) (KYC norms), Q5(c) (Clauses 7 and 8 Part I Second Schedule) and Q6(c) EITHER (Council General Guidelines 2008, Chapter IX), plus MCQs 9 and 11.

**2. Ch 7 Reporting** (35 marks set of 354). Opinion and report forms plus CARO 2020. ICAI asks (i) which modified opinion fits a stated limitation or misstatement (SA 705), often with a drafted Opinion/Basis paragraph (Jan 2026 Q1(a), compulsory), (ii) Emphasis of Matter vs Other Matter vs KAM, and (iii) specific CARO clauses (CSR unspent, revaluation, clause (xxi), cash losses, whistle-blower, term loans, Sections 185/186). Marks: 4-5 for CARO or drafting questions, 2 per MCQ. Ch 7 held the compulsory Q1(c) in Sep 2025 (4) and Q1(a) in Jan 2026 (5), and Q5(a) in May 2026.

**3. Ch 14 Special Features of Audit of Banks and NBFCs** (27 marks set of 354). Never in Q1 in these attempts, so always a choice area; asked as RBI prudential-norm applications. Recurring: asset classification and provisioning (restructuring Sep 2025 Q4(a); NBFC NPA with secured portion Jan 2026 MCQ 8; renewal and NPA May 2026 MCQ 14, Q3(a)), NBFC capital/NOF/liquid assets (MCQs Sep 2025 and Jan 2026), and bank-audit procedures (revenue leakage, investments). Includes numerical MCQs (risk-weighted assets, provisions). Typical marks 5 for the descriptive part, 2 per MCQ.

**4. Ch 2 General Auditing Principles and Auditor's Responsibilities** (25 marks set of 354). SA 240 fraud, SA 250 laws and regulations, SA 260/299/402. Questions give a scenario and ask for the auditor's responsibility or required action: fraud risk factors (Jan 2026 Q5(b)), withdrawal on fraud (Jan 2026 Q6(c) OR), laws and regulations (Sep 2025 Q2(a); May 2026 MCQ 2), significant findings to TCWG (May 2026 Q2(a)), joint-audit responsibility (Sep 2025 MCQ 12), service-organisation Type 2 report (May 2026 MCQ 7). Never in Q1. Typically 5 marks descriptive, 2 per MCQ.

**5. Ch 5 Audit Evidence** (19 marks set of 354). SA 505/501/500 style evidence questions: reliability of confirmation responses (Sep 2025 Q5(b)), stock count (Jan 2026 MCQ 2), litigation confirmation with SA 580/501 (May 2026 Q1(a), compulsory), management's expert under SA 500 (May 2026 Q4(a)), SA 550 related parties (Jan 2026 MCQ 3). Typically a 5-mark scenario question in each attempt.

**6. Ch 18 SDG and ESG Assurance** (18 marks set of 354). Exactly 6 marks in each attempt, never in Q1. Forms: BRSR principles and indicators (Jan 2026 MCQ 14; May 2026 MCQ 10, Q4(c)), BRSR assurance (Sep 2025 MCQ 13), ESG pillar elements (Sep 2025 Q4(c)), Integrated Reporting capitals (Jan 2026 Q4(c)). The descriptive part is a 4-mark question; the rest is 2-mark MCQs.

**7. Ch 10 Review of Financial Information** (18 marks set of 354). SRE 2400 and SRE 2410: objective (Sep 2025 Q3(c)), written representations (Sep 2025 Q6(b); May 2026 Q4(b)), analytical procedures (Jan 2026 Q2(c)). Always descriptive, 4-5 marks, never in Q1, never as MCQ in these attempts.

**8. Ch 12 Digital Auditing and Assurance** (17 marks set of 354). Digital topics are tested as one descriptive question (remote audit Sep 2025 Q4(b), RPA Jan 2026 Q3(c), cybersecurity risk assessment under SA 315, May 2026 Q2(c)) plus a cyber MCQ (Jan 2026 MCQ 13 DoS; May 2026 MCQ 6 social engineering). 4-5 marks descriptive, 2 per MCQ.

**9. Ch 3 Audit Planning, Strategy and Execution** (16 marks set of 354). Execution standards that ICAI picks one at a time: SA 540 estimation uncertainty (Sep 2025 Q3(b)), SA 450 uncorrected misstatements (Jan 2026 Q1(b), compulsory), SA 620 expert (Sep 2025 MCQ 10; May 2026 MCQ 5), SA 610 internal auditors (May 2026 MCQ 8). Form: "guide / evaluate with reference to the SA", 5 marks, plus 2-mark MCQs.

**10. Ch 17 Due Diligence, Investigation and Forensic Accounting** (16 marks set of 354). Due diligence steps (Jan 2026 Q6(a)), investigating commission-scheme fraud (Sep 2025 Q6(c) OR), forensic vs audit (May 2026 MCQ 12) and investigating accountant's evaluation of fixed assets (May 2026 Q6(b)). Mostly the Q6 slot; 4-5 marks.
