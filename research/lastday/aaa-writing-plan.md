## Hand and format checklist (app-written)

- Posture and grip: relaxed grip, forearm resting on the desk, shake out the hand every 20-25 minutes; use the same pen type you will use in the exam, and carry spares.
- Question number written legibly at the start of every answer (the paper's own instruction says this), sub-part labels as printed, e.g. Q2(b)(i).
- Three-step layout for scenario answers: (1) provision (the clause / SA / section, quoted by number); (2) analysis (apply it to the facts given, using the names in the question); (3) conclusion (one line, answering exactly what was asked).
- Underline SA numbers, clause numbers and Schedule references; leave a line between sub-parts.
- Working notes form part of the answer (printed on the paper); keep them under the answer.
- Marks drive length: about 9 minutes and roughly 20-25 lines for 5 marks; about 7 minutes and 15-18 lines for 4 marks. (Line counts are an app-written rule of thumb, adjust to your handwriting.)
- Speed: after each COPY or MEMORY exercise write down actual minutes taken next to the yardstick; if you are over by more than 20%, shorten the analysis, never skip the conclusion.
- Never leave a question blank; write the provision and the conclusion even if the analysis is short.
