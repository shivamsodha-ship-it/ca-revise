## How marks are counted (student's correction applied)

Every question's marks are recorded exactly as printed. Each paper **sets** more than 100 marks, because Part II has six questions and the candidate does Q1 plus any four of Q2 to Q6. A candidate **attempts 100 marks**: 30 (Part I MCQs, all compulsory) + Q1 (14, compulsory) + any 4 of the 5 choice questions (4 x 14 = 56).

So a topic in Part I or Q1 is always attempted; a topic that sits only in Q2 to Q6 can be skipped. In the tables, rows are marked **Compulsory (Part I)**, **Compulsory (Q1)** or **Choice**.
