# AFM writing-practice plan, 26 Oct to 3 Nov 2026

Exam: 4 Nov 2026. All marks, question labels and question text are from the ICAI papers. The schedule, timings, concept labels, formula drills and checklist are **app-written plan**. Nothing here restates question text: the app shows the full question from the ICAI file named in the last table.

## Assumptions and rules

- **Assumption (scale as needed):** 90 minutes of AFM handwriting per day, in two blocks of 45 minutes (Block A, Block B). On 3 Nov the plan is a lighter 60 minutes (two blocks of 30).
- **Timing yardstick:** 1.8 minutes per mark (ICAI: 100 marks in 180 minutes). Minutes per exercise = marks x 1.8, rounded to the nearest minute (4 marks = 7, 5 = 9, 6 = 11, 7 = 13, 8 = 14, 10 = 18).
- Each block starts with an 8-minute formula drill (write the key formulas of that day's chapter from memory, then check against the SM chapter text in `work/sm/afm/`). The remaining minutes in a block are 'compare and tidy' time.
- **Modes:** *copy* = copy the ICAI model answer neatly (formula first, then substitution, units, boxed result). *from-memory* = read the question and the ICAI answer, close it, write it again, compare. *timed* = blank sheet, 1.8 min per mark, then compare. *full-paper* = whole ICAI-style paper under the clock.
- **Days 7 and 8 are full 3-hour papers** (180 minutes, Nov 2026 MTP Series I and II), which is an exception to the 90-minute day. If 3 hours is not available, do the 90-minute sectional given under each day (50 marks x 1.8 = 90 minutes). Marking against the ICAI answers is done separately and is not counted.
- May 2026 questions have no ICAI suggested answer yet, so on those rows the 'compare' step uses the SM chapter or an earlier ICAI answer that uses the same method (named in the row). RTPs print no marks, so no RTP question is used in the timed plan.
- Priority follows the analysis (afm-last3-analysis.md): Ch 9 (45 marks), Ch 10 (41), Ch 6 (38), Ch 8 (37), Ch 5 (34), Ch 14 (29), Ch 11 (25), Ch 12 (21). Repeat codes R1 to R19 refer to the repeat list there.

## Hand and format checklist (app-written)

- Question number written legibly in the margin and at the start of each answer; sub-part labels (a), (i) kept.
- Working notes labelled and kept with the answer; one clean table per working, ruled columns, total line underlined.
- Formula first, then substitution, then result; units (` lakh, crore, %, per unit) on every figure.
- Final answer boxed or underlined; the decision sentence (buy/hold/sell, which option, accept/reject) written after the box.
- Leave 2 blank lines between sub-parts; do not squeeze, so later corrections fit.
- Write at your natural speed, not faster than you can read back; check figures copied from the question once.
- Use the same pen and writing position as in the exam; sit upright; relax the grip every 20 minutes.
- Round only at the end unless the question says otherwise; follow the stated decimal places.
- Theory answers: short numbered points, one line each, underline the key term.
