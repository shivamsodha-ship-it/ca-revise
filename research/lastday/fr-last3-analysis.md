**How marks work (important).** Each paper prints Part I = 15 MCQs x 2 = 30 marks, all compulsory. Part II prints Q1 (14, compulsory) plus five choice questions Q2-Q6 (14 each); a candidate answers Q1 + any 4 of Q2-Q6. So a candidate **attempts 100 marks** (30 + 14 + 4 x 14), but the **marks set on the paper** are larger. Weightage below is on *marks set*. A topic in Part I or Q1 is certain to be attempted (marked **Compulsory**); a topic in Q2-Q6 is attempted only if the student chooses that question (marked Choice).
