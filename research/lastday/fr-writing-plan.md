# FR: writing-practice plan, 26 Oct to 1 Nov 2026 (7 days)

**Everything in this file that is not ICAI text is app-written plan** (headings, schedule, time budget, checklist). Every exercise below is a real ICAI question; the app shows the full question text from the ICAI file named in the source index. No question text is rewritten here.

## Assumptions (app-written, scale them)

- 90 minutes of FR handwriting per day, in two blocks (A and B, about 45 minutes each). Change the minutes if your day is shorter or longer; keep the ratio.
- Timing yardstick: ICAI papers give 100 marks in 3 hours, so **1.8 minutes per mark** (a 6-mark answer is about 11 minutes; 14 marks is about 25 minutes). Minutes in the table are marks x 1.8, rounded. A 'compare' line is extra time after the writing; each day's total is within a few minutes of 90, and Day 7 is deliberately about 40.
- Modes: **copy** = copy the ICAI model answer neatly (warm the hand, learn the layout); **from-memory** = same question again with the ICAI answer closed, then compare in a different colour; **timed** = a question you have not seen, stopped at the minutes shown, then compare.
- Marks are as printed in the ICAI paper. May 2026 questions are used now that ICAI has published the suggested answers (`work/papers/fr_may26_sa_1.txt`) to compare with; the RTP is not used because its questions carry no printed marks. MCQs are not written practice.
- Priority follows the analysis file (`research/lastday/fr-last3-analysis.md`): consolidated balance sheet (compulsory Q1 every paper) gets five exercises; compound instruments five; Ind AS 19 four; Ind AS 20 four; then Ind AS 115, 101, 36, 23, 33, 113, 8, 1, 34, 105, 21 and the Ethics case.

## Hand and format checklist (app-written)

1. **Working note.** Heading 'W.N. 1: Goodwill / NCI / Other equity' on its own line, underlined; one column of labels, one column of figures; rule off the total with a single line, double line under the final answer. Refer to it in the main answer as '(W.N. 1)'. ICAI says working notes form part of the answer.
2. **Journal entry.** Dr line at the left margin, 'To' line indented, amounts in the right column, one-line narration '(Being ...)'. One entry per event; leave one blank line between entries.
3. **Table.** Rule the columns with a pencil/ruler before writing; put the year or party in the header; write the unit once (` in lakhs`).
4. **Two-line theory answer.** Line 1: rule or principle with the Ind AS number and paragraph if you are sure of it; line 2: apply it to the facts and state the conclusion. Do not write a paragraph where two lines answer the mark.
5. **Margin for marks.** Leave a 3 cm margin on the right. Put the marks you expect for each sub-part in the margin; if you have written more lines than marks, stop.
6. **Stopping at the time limit.** Note the clock time at the start of each question; at 1.8 minutes per mark, stop when the time is up even mid-working, write 'balance working similarly' only if the method is already shown, and move on. Come back only after all questions are attempted.
7. **Readable numbers.** Write 4, 7 and 9 distinctly; use full stops for decimals; write units; never overwrite, strike through and rewrite.
8. **Question number.** Write the question number legibly (ICAI instructs this) and start each question on a fresh line.
